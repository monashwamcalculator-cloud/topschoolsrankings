Write for Us: Guest Post Guidelines

Share your educational expertise, student experiences, and admission advice with millions of readers.

Welcome to the Top Schools Rankings contributor page! If you are passionate about education, student life, university admissions, or career planning, you are in the right place. We are always on the lookout for talented writers, seasoned educators, and current students who want to share their unique insights with our growing, global community.

Our platform reaches thousands of high school students, college applicants, and parents every single month. By contributing a guest post to Top Schools Rankings, you have the opportunity to make a tangible impact on a student's educational journey, build your own professional portfolio, and connect with a highly engaged audience.

Before you submit your pitch or article, please read through this comprehensive guide. It outlines exactly what we are looking for, our editorial standards, the topics that resonate most with our audience, and the step-by-step submission process.

Who We Are Looking For

We believe that the best advice comes from diverse perspectives. We do not require you to be a professional freelance writer to contribute, but we do require you to have genuine knowledge and passion for your topic. We regularly publish articles from:

  • Current Students & Alumni: Are you currently studying at a university or boarding school? We want your first-hand experiences! Share what campus life is actually like, how you survived finals week, or the truth about your university's dorm food.
  • Admissions Counselors & Consultants: Share your professional expertise on navigating the complex world of college applications, securing financial aid, or acing the interview process.
  • Educators & Teachers: Provide study tips, curriculum breakdowns (like IB vs. AP), and advice on academic success.
  • Industry Professionals: If you are a graduate now working in your dream career, our readers want to know how you got there. What degree did you pursue? What internships were crucial?
  • Passionate Education Writers: Even if you don't fall into the categories above, if you have meticulously researched a topic related to global education, we want to hear from you.

Topics We Love to Publish

Our goal is to provide actionable, evidence-based, and highly engaging content. If you want your pitch to be accepted quickly, consider writing about one of our core pillars:

1. University & College Admissions

The application process is incredibly stressful. Articles that demystify this process perform exceptionally well. Examples of topics include:

  • How to write a compelling personal statement for Ivy League schools.
  • The ultimate timeline for high school juniors preparing for college.
  • Understanding Early Action vs. Early Decision.
  • Tips for international students applying to UK, US, Canada, or Australian universities.

2. Standardized Testing (SAT, ACT, IELTS, PTE, GMAT)

Test prep is a massive part of a student's journey. We are looking for strategies, study plans, and resource guides:

  • How I increased my SAT score by 200 points in three months.
  • PTE vs. IELTS: Which English proficiency test is right for you?
  • Last-minute ACT tips for test day.

3. Student Life & Campus Guides

Students want to know what it's actually like to live and study at a specific school. These posts should be highly relatable and authentic:

  • The honest truth about Greek Life at large state universities.
  • A day in the life of a computer science major at MIT.
  • How to manage your mental health and avoid burnout during midterms.
  • Dorm room essentials: What you actually need (and what you should leave at home).

4. Scholarships & Financial Aid

Education is expensive. Content that helps students save money is always in high demand:

  • A step-by-step guide to filling out the FAFSA.
  • Little-known scholarships for international students.
  • How to negotiate your financial aid package with the admissions office.

5. School Rankings & Comparisons

Our brand is built on helping students compare options. If you want to write a comparison piece, it must be objective, data-driven, and highly detailed:

  • Public vs. Private High Schools: Weighing the pros and cons.
  • The top 10 liberal arts colleges for pre-med students.
  • Comparing the IB Diploma program with the British A-Level system.

Our Strict Editorial Guidelines (Must Read)

To maintain the high quality of Top Schools Rankings, all submissions must adhere strictly to our editorial guidelines. Articles that fail to meet these standards will be rejected without feedback.

1. Originality and Plagiarism

We have a zero-tolerance policy for plagiarism. Your submission must be 100% original and written exclusively for Top Schools Rankings. We use advanced plagiarism detection software. If you submit copied content, you will be permanently banned from contributing. You may not republish the article on your own blog or Medium account after we publish it.

2. AI-Generated Content Policy

While we acknowledge that AI tools (like ChatGPT or Claude) can be useful for brainstorming outlines or checking grammar, we do not accept fully AI-generated articles. Your article must contain human insight, personal anecdotes, unique opinions, and a conversational tone that an AI cannot replicate. We run all submissions through AI-detection tools. Articles that read like robotic, generic encyclopedias will be rejected.

3. Word Count and Depth

We prioritize depth over breadth. We do not publish short, 500-word fluff pieces. Your article must be comprehensive. The minimum word count is 1,200 words, but our most successful articles are usually between 1,500 and 2,500 words. Go deep into your topic. Use examples, data, quotes, and actionable advice.

4. Formatting and Readability

Online readers scan. Your article must be easily scannable and visually appealing. Please follow these formatting rules:

  • Use H2 and H3 headings to break up the text logically.
  • Keep paragraphs short (no more than 3-4 sentences per paragraph).
  • Use bulleted or numbered lists wherever possible to present information clearly.
  • Bold important concepts or key takeaways.

5. Linking Policy (External and Internal)

We encourage you to cite your sources! If you mention a statistic or a specific university policy, link to the official primary source (e.g., the university's official website or a government data portal).

Self-Promotion: You are allowed one do-follow link to your own personal blog, portfolio, or non-competing educational business within your author bio. You may also include links to your social media profiles (LinkedIn, Twitter). We do NOT allow promotional links in the body of the article unless previously agreed upon via a sponsored post arrangement. Links to spammy, gambling, or irrelevant sites will result in immediate rejection.

Internal Linking: Please try to link to at least two existing articles on Top Schools Rankings within your text. This shows us that you have actually read our site.

6. Tone of Voice

Our tone is authoritative yet accessible, encouraging, and empathetic. Speak directly to the reader (use "you" and "your"). Avoid overly dense academic jargon unless you are clearly defining it. We want to be the helpful older sibling or the trusted advisor for our readers.

The Submission Process: How to Pitch Us

We process a large volume of submissions, so following this process is crucial for a timely response.

Step 1: Send a Pitch

Do not send a completed manuscript right away. Instead, send an email to topschoolsrankings@gmail.com with the subject line: Guest Post Pitch: [Your Proposed Title].

In your email, include:

  • A brief introduction of who you are and why you are qualified to write on this topic.
  • Links to 2-3 writing samples (published articles or a Google Doc).
  • Three proposed headlines/topics.
  • A brief outline (3-4 bullet points) for your favorite topic.

Step 2: Get Approval

Our editorial team will review your pitch within 5-7 business days. If we like your idea, we will reply with the green light, along with any specific SEO keywords we'd like you to include or slight tweaks to the angle.

Step 3: Write and Submit

Once approved, write your masterpiece! Submit your final draft via a Google Doc link with editing permissions enabled. Ensure that you have thoroughly proofread your work using Grammarly or a similar tool. At the bottom of the document, include your Author Bio (50-75 words), a link to your headshot, and your chosen backlink.

Step 4: The Editing Phase

Our editors will review your draft. We reserve the right to edit your article for clarity, grammar, SEO optimization, and formatting. If significant changes are needed, we will leave comments in the Google Doc and ask you to revise.

Step 5: Publication

Once the article is finalized, we will schedule it for publication! We will email you the live link so you can share it with your network.

What Are the Benefits of Writing for Us?

Why should you spend hours crafting an amazing article for Top Schools Rankings? Here is what you get in return:

  • Massive Exposure: Your work will be read by an audience of highly motivated students, parents, and educators worldwide.
  • Professional Credibility: Having a published article on a reputable education platform instantly boosts your resume and establishes you as a thought leader in your niche.
  • A Valuable Backlink: We provide a do-follow link to your website or portfolio in your author bio, which is excellent for your own site's SEO.
  • Social Media Promotion: We share our top guest posts across our social media channels, giving your work an extra boost of traffic.
  • A Dedicated Author Page: Frequent contributors will receive their own dedicated author archive page on our site.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do you pay for guest posts?

At this time, we do not offer monetary compensation for unsolicited guest posts. The primary benefits are exposure, portfolio building, and the author bio backlink. If you are a professional freelance writer looking for a paid, ongoing gig, please check our "Careers" page for any open staff writer positions.

Can I submit an article that has been published elsewhere?

No. We only accept 100% original, unpublished content. If we find that the article exists elsewhere on the web, we will take it down immediately.

How long does it take to get published?

Depending on our editorial calendar, it usually takes 1 to 3 weeks from the time you submit your final draft to the time it goes live on the site.

Do I need to provide images?

You are not required to provide featured images; our design team handles that to ensure visual consistency across the site. However, if your article relies on specific charts, graphs, or screenshots (especially for tutorials), please include them in your Google Doc as high-resolution attachments.

Will you change my article?

Yes. Our editorial team will likely make minor tweaks for SEO, readability, and formatting. We may change the headline to improve click-through rates. However, we will never alter the core message or your personal opinions.

Ready to Start Writing?

We are thrilled that you want to contribute to the Top Schools Rankings community. Your knowledge could be exactly what a stressed-out high school senior needs to read right now.

Take some time to browse our existing Guides and Listings to get a feel for our style. When you have a brilliant idea, send your pitch to topschoolsrankings@gmail.com.

We can't wait to read what you create!
