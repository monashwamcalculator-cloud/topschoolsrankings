<div class="answer-box"><span>Quick answer</span><p>Choose a UK course by comparing its modules, teaching, assessment, placement options, entry requirements, total cost and official Discover Uni data. Courses with the same title may offer substantially different learning experiences.</p></div><div class="editor-note"><strong>Editorial note:</strong> Education, admissions, fees and immigration rules can change. Check the linked official source again before applying or paying.</div><section><h2>Define the subject outcome you want</h2><p>Begin with the knowledge or career direction you want rather than a university name. Identify required topics, optional specialisms and whether you need professional accreditation. UCAS notes that courses sharing a title can vary in content, so the module list is one of the first documents to compare.</p></section><section><h2>Compare the course structure line by line</h2><p>Save the course specification rather than relying on a summary page. Ask which modules are guaranteed and which depend on demand or staffing.</p><ul><li>Compulsory and optional modules for each year</li><li>Lectures, seminars, laboratories, studios and independent study</li><li>Exams, coursework, practical work, presentations and group assessment</li><li>Placement, year-abroad or integrated master’s options</li><li>Rules for changing pathways after enrolment</li></ul></section><section><h2>Use Discover Uni as evidence, not a verdict</h2><p>Discover Uni lets applicants compare official course data and explains where the information comes from. Review student experience, continuation and graduate outcomes together, paying attention to the cohort, response rate and any note that data has been grouped from related courses.</p><p>A percentage does not tell the whole story. Use it to form a question for the department—for example, how assessment feedback works or which careers recent graduates entered.</p></section><section><h2>Check entry and offer conditions</h2><p>Compare required subjects, grade ranges, English-language evidence, portfolio or interview requirements and any contextual-admissions information. For international qualifications, use the university’s country page and request written clarification if your curriculum is not listed.</p><p>Do not assume that meeting the published minimum guarantees an offer. Competitive selection, subject combinations and applicant volume may also matter.</p></section><section><h2>Test the practical fit</h2><ul><li>Can you afford the full course and location under a stress-test budget?</li><li>Does the teaching and assessment style suit how you learn?</li><li>Are disability, wellbeing and academic-support services accessible to you?</li><li>Is accommodation available for your applicant category?</li><li>What careers support is embedded in the department rather than only university-wide?</li></ul></section><section><h2>Create a final evidence table</h2><p>For each course, record the official link, date checked, module fit, cost, entry risk, outcome evidence and two unanswered questions. Score only after the evidence is complete. A transparent table makes it easier to explain the choice to yourself and prevents reputation from replacing research.</p></section><section class="faq-section"><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><details><summary>Are courses with the same title equivalent?</summary><p>No. Modules, assessment, teaching hours, placements and professional recognition can differ substantially.</p></details><details><summary>Is Discover Uni a university ranking?</summary><p>It is an official course-information and comparison service. Its data should be interpreted with the published context and limitations.</p></details><details><summary>Should I choose the course or university first?</summary><p>For most applicants, define the subject and course requirements first, then compare the institutions that meet them.</p></details></section><section class="source-section"><h2>Primary sources</h2><p>Sources were accessed for this guide on 20 August 2026.</p><ul><li><a href="https://www.ucas.com/applying/you-apply/what-and-where-study/choosing-course" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">UCAS choosing a course</a></li><li><a href="https://www.ucas.com/applying/before-you-apply/what-and-where-to-study/choosing-a-course/comparing-undergraduate-courses-and-unis" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">UCAS comparing courses and universities</a></li><li><a href="https://discoveruni.gov.uk/course-comparison/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Discover Uni course comparison</a></li><li><a href="https://discoveruni.gov.uk/about-our-data/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Discover Uni about its data</a></li></ul></section>
