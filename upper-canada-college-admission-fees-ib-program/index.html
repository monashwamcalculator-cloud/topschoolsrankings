Upper Canada College: Admission, Fees & IB Program Guide 2026

Discover everything about Upper Canada College in Toronto: admission requirements, tuition fees ($34,135-$73,700), IB program, boarding options.

Are you looking for the best private boys' school in Toronto for your child? Finding the right educational institution is one of the most important decisions parents make, especially when considering elite international education options in Canada.

Upper Canada College (UCC) stands as Toronto's foremost independent all-boys day and boarding school, offering world-class education through the rigorous International Baccalaureate (IB) program. Founded in 1829, this prestigious institution has educated generations of global leaders, history-makers, and innovators.

In this comprehensive guide, you'll discover everything about Upper Canada College: detailed admission requirements, tuition fees (ranging from $34,135 to $73,700 CAD), the complete IB curriculum structure, boarding options for international students, and the application process. Whether you are applying from the UK, Australia, the USA, or anywhere else globally, this information will help you make an informed decision for your son's academic future.

About Upper Canada College

Historical Background

Upper Canada College was founded in 1829 by Sir John Colborne, the Lieutenant Governor of Upper Canada. The school was meticulously modelled after Britain's top historic all-boys schools, most notably Eton College in England. This rich historical foundation continues to heavily shape UCC's structured educational philosophy today.

The original campus was located in Russell Square downtown before moving to its current premium 17-hectare site in Deer Park (Midtown Toronto) in 1891. In 1900, when the Ontario government stopped public funding, UCC became completely independent. Today, it remains proudly as the oldest independent school in the province of Ontario.

Academic Programs & IB Curriculum

The Complete IB Continuum

Upper Canada College is highly unique as the only all-boys school across North America to offer all three tiers of the International Baccalaureate programs. This integrated pathway ensures a smooth intellectual transition from early childhood to university preparation.

1. IB Primary Years Programme (PYP)

  • Grades: Senior Kindergarten to Year 5 (Ages 4–11)

  • Focus: Holistic development, inquiry-based learning frameworks, and sparking early intellectual curiosity across foundational subjects.

2. IB Middle Years Programme (MYP)

  • Grades: Years 6 to 10 (Grade 6–10)

  • Focus: Critical thinking, intercultural awareness, and real-world problem-solving. It includes specialized design courses focusing on coding, digital media, and innovative product design.

3. IB Diploma Programme (DP)

  • Grades: Years 11 and 12 (Grade 11–12)

  • Structure: A rigorous pre-university curriculum combining 6 distinct IB subject groups alongside the core requirements: Theory of Knowledge (TOK), the Extended Essay (EE), and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS).

The Dual Diploma Advantage

Every graduating student at UCC concurrently earns two distinct certifications:

  1. The globally recognized International Baccalaureate Diploma.

  2. The provincial Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD).

This dual qualification gives graduates unparalleled flexibility and an immense competitive advantage when applying to top-tier universities across Canada, the US, the UK, Europe, and Australia.

Academic Support & Language Frameworks

UCC provides specialized, top-tier academic support through its dedicated on-campus Learning Centres. Faculty experts offer individual advising, study skills workshops, and targeted math and writing support. For international students, UCC integrates robust English Language Learning (ELL) support directly into the curriculum, ensuring non-native speakers quickly master academic writing and presentation skills.

Admission Requirements & Process

Core Entry Levels

While applications are evaluated based on availability, day students are primarily accepted at these standard entry points: Junior Kindergarten (JK), Year 3 (Grade 2), Year 5 (Grade 4), Year 6 (Grade 5), Year 7 (Grade 6), and Year 9 (Grade 8). The Boarding Program officially opens for students entering Years 8 through 12 (Grades 7–12).

Required Application Documents

To apply, parents must complete the online portal requirements and submit:

  • Copies of the student's two most recent final school report cards.

  • Official birth verification (copy of passport or birth certificate).

  • A recent high-resolution photograph of the applicant.

  • SSAT Entrance Test scores (Strictly required for applicants entering Grade 7 and above).

  • A personalized interactive interview with the admissions team (which accounts for a significant portion of the final evaluation profile).

Admissions Competitive Ratio: The general acceptance ratio sits at approximately 4:1 (four applicants for every one available seat), making the entry process highly competitive.

One-Time Fees & Ancillary Costs

Upon formal acceptance, families must pay a one-time, non-refundable registration fee: $8,500 CAD for day students or $5,000 CAD for boarding students. Additional yearly costs include textbooks and software supplies (~$1,500–$2,500 CAD), initial uniforms (~$1,000–$2,000 CAD), and athletic or experiential trips.

The Financial Assistance Program

UCC runs one of the most generous financial aid programs in the country, recognizing that less than 2% of families can comfortably manage full independent fees on their own.

  • Total Annual Budget: Over $5 million CAD awarded based purely on demonstrated financial need.

  • Student Inclusions: Roughly 20% of the entire student body receives partial to substantial aid.

  • Median Value: The median aid package rests at approximately $15,000 CAD per year.

Boarding Program for International Students

The Global Boarding Environment

UCC provides a safe, structured residential home for students from all corners of the globe. International boarders are placed into distinct, closely knit on-campus residential houses under 24/7 expert adult supervision.

A Day in the Life of a UCC Boarder

Life in the boarding houses is highly structured to balance focus with personal freedom. A typical weekday follows a precise rhythm:

  • 7:30 AM – 8:15 AM: Group breakfast in the main dining hall.

  • 8:30 AM – 3:30 PM: Academic classes and school day activities.

  • 3:45 PM – 5:30 PM: Mandatory sports practices, arts clubs, or fitness training.

  • 6:00 PM: Family-style dinner with resident masters and peers.

  • 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM: Mandatory, quiet evening study hours inside the study centres with direct tutoring support.

  • Weekends: Organized trips across Toronto, including major sporting events, museum visits, ski excursions, and supervised social time.

Complete Custodianship & Visa Support

For international families, the school ensures full compliance with Canadian immigration codes by providing official Custodianship Declaration services for study permit processing. From airport shuttle pickups and medical insurance set-up to coordinating local homestay breaks when dorms close, international students receive end-to-end operational care.

Facilities & Student Life

Campus Infrastructure

The massive 17-hectare urban campus rivals many small universities, featuring:

  • Advanced science, chemistry, and biology experimental laboratories.

  • A state-of-the-art indoor swimming pool, multiple double-court gymnasiums, a fitness arena, and immaculate outdoor rugby and soccer fields.

  • Dedicated music wings, creative art studios, and a fully equipped drama theatre.

Community Service and Co-Curricular Mandates

UCC emphasizes character just as much as grades. To graduate, every student must complete the standard 40 hours of community service required by the OSSD, plus an additional 150 hours of co-curricular activities integrated within the IB profile.

UCC's athletic programs have a legacy of excellence, producing multiple Olympic medalists across sports like rowing and rugby, including legends James Elder, Michael Evans, George Mara, and Barney Williams.

Why Choose Upper Canada College?

Selecting UCC means investing in an unmatched educational network. The lifelong connection to the UCC Alumni Network offers students career mentorship from prominent global figures, including business titans Ted Rogers (Rogers Communications), David Thomson (Thomson Corporation), Galen Weston, and award-winning artists like Leonard Dick and Galt MacDermot.

An IB Diploma from UCC is a passport to the world’s elite universities, with graduates routinely receiving direct placement offers from Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Oxford, Cambridge, the University of Toronto, and McGill.

Conclusion

Upper Canada College represents the undisputed gold standard for all-boys private education in Canada. By perfectly bridging nearly two centuries of rich institutional history with the world's most modern academic framework, UCC creates an environment where boys grow into highly competent, global-minded leaders.

FAQs

1. Is Upper Canada College only for boarders?
No. UCC is a combined day and boarding school. Day programs start at Senior Kindergarten, while the boarding program accommodates local and international students from Grade 7 through Grade 12.

2. What is the real cost of attending UCC for an international boarding student?
The all-inclusive international boarding tuition fee ranges between $66,835 and $73,700 CAD annually, depending on the exact grade level, plus a one-time $5,000 CAD boarding registration fee.

3. Does UCC offer support for non-native English speakers?
Yes. UCC provides integrated English Language Learning (ELL) support models alongside dedicated learning specialists to ensure all international boarders find academic success.
