How the Top 100 Schools in India Ranking Was Compiled

The 2025-26 EducationWorld India School Rankings are based on a perception-based survey of school education stakeholders. EducationWorld states that AZ Research Partners conducted interviews with 9,500 respondents including parents, principals, teachers and educationists.

For day-school rankings, respondents rated schools across 14 parameters of school education excellence. These include areas such as academic reputation, curriculum and pedagogy, co-curricular education, faculty competence, leadership, infrastructure, value for money and teacher welfare.

This methodology means that the ranking reflects stakeholder perceptions across multiple dimensions of school education rather than being based only on examination results or board scores.

School Boards and Curricula

School board is another factor that deserves attention when comparing schools. Depending on the institution, families may encounter CBSE, CISCE/ICSE, international curricula and other educational approaches.

The curriculum affects classroom structure, assessment patterns, subject choices and the way students progress through secondary and senior secondary education. A school appearing high in a national ranking does not automatically mean its curriculum will be the right fit for every student.

Before applying, families should check the school's current official curriculum, board affiliation, subject availability and grade-level requirements rather than relying only on a ranking table.

What Makes a School Worth Considering?

A national ranking can help create a shortlist, but several practical factors can have a greater impact on a student's everyday experience.

  • Academic approach: Look at curriculum, teaching methods, assessment practices and academic support.
  • Faculty: Consider teacher experience, stability and the level of academic and pastoral support available.
  • Infrastructure: Review classrooms, laboratories, libraries, sports facilities and other facilities relevant to the student's interests.
  • Co-curricular opportunities: Check options for sports, arts, music, clubs, competitions and other activities.
  • Location: For a day school, daily commute can make a significant difference to a student's routine.
  • Fees: Compare tuition fees with transportation, activity, admission and other recurring charges.
  • Admission availability: Check entry grades, application deadlines, age requirements and seat availability.
  • School environment: A campus visit can provide useful context that a ranking cannot capture.

How to Choose a School From a National Ranking

The most practical approach is to use a national ranking as the first stage of research rather than the final decision.

  1. Shortlist schools that are accessible from your location.
  2. Check the curriculum and board offered by each school.
  3. Compare the complete fee structure, not only the advertised tuition fee.
  4. Review admission requirements and application timelines.
  5. Visit the shortlisted campuses where possible.
  6. Ask about class size, student support, activities and transportation.
  7. Compare the school's current information with the ranking year.

This approach helps turn a large national list into a smaller group of schools that are actually relevant to the student's needs.

School Fees in India

There is no single fee range that applies to the schools in this ranking. Fees vary considerably by city, school type, curriculum, grade level and facilities.

The published tuition fee may also be only one part of the total annual cost. Families may need to account for admission charges, transportation, meals, books, uniforms, activities, technology charges, trips and other school-specific expenses.

School Admission Process in India

Admission procedures differ from one school to another. Some schools have multiple entry points, while others have limited seats at particular grade levels.

A typical process may include registration, submission of documents, interaction or assessment, parent interaction, selection and fee payment. The exact procedure depends on the school and the grade for which admission is being sought.

Documents Commonly Requested

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school records
  • Transfer certificate where applicable
  • Recent photographs
  • Address proof
  • Identity documents
  • Medical or vaccination records where required

These requirements are not universal. Always check the school's current admission notice before submitting an application.

National Ranking vs City Ranking

For day schools, geography matters. A school may have a strong national position, but a family usually needs to consider travel time, school transport, daily schedule and accessibility as well.

EducationWorld itself notes that national ranking has limited practical value for day scholars compared with city or town rankings, where access is easier to assess. National rankings remain useful for broad benchmarking and initial research.

This is particularly relevant in large metropolitan areas where a student's daily commute can vary significantly even between schools located within the same broader city.

Are School Rankings Enough to Choose a School?

No single ranking can capture every part of a student's school experience.

Rankings can help identify established schools and provide a structured comparison. They can also make it easier to discover institutions that may otherwise be missed during initial research.

However, a ranking does not tell a family everything about classroom culture, teacher-student interaction, commute, current vacancies, individual learning support or whether the school's educational approach matches a particular student's needs.

The most useful role of a ranking is therefore to help build a shortlist. The final comparison should include current school information, fees, curriculum, location, admission requirements and a campus visit where possible.

Why School Rankings Change Every Year

School rankings can move from one year to another for several reasons. Changes in survey methodology, respondent composition, school performance, institutional development and the set of schools included in a category can all affect the final table.

For the 2025-26 EducationWorld rankings, the research process involved a broader respondent database and 9,500 respondents. EducationWorld also introduced a separate Ivy League category for schools that had repeatedly ranked highly in earlier editions. These changes mean that year-to-year movements should be interpreted in the context of the methodology used for that particular edition.

How to Verify a School Before Applying

Once a school has been shortlisted, verify the information that can change during the year.

  • Current fee structure
  • Admission dates and available grades
  • Board affiliation
  • Current curriculum
  • Transportation routes
  • Age and eligibility requirements
  • Campus facilities
  • Academic calendar
  • Official contact details

Ranking tables are useful for comparison, but the school's own current admission information should be used for application-related decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the #1 school in India in the 2025-26 co-ed day school ranking?

The Shri Ram School, Vasant Vihar/Moulsari, Delhi is ranked #1 in the EducationWorld 2025-26 Best Co-ed Day Schools national table, with a total score of 1,317.

What does the Top 100 Schools in India list represent?

This list represents the first 100 school entries in EducationWorld's 2025-26 national ranking for Best Co-ed Day Schools. It is not a universal ranking covering every school type, curriculum or school category in India.

How many schools are included in the EducationWorld co-ed day ranking?

EducationWorld states that more than 3,000 co-ed day schools are ranked nationally, across states and cities. The national table presented for the Top 100 includes the leading 100 school entries.

How is the EducationWorld school ranking calculated?

For the 2025-26 rankings, EducationWorld states that 9,500 respondents including parents, principals, teachers and educationists participated in the research process. Day schools were evaluated across 14 parameters of school education excellence.

Does a higher national rank mean the school is better for every student?

No. A ranking reflects the methodology and respondent perceptions used to create that particular table. Curriculum, location, fees, admission requirements, school environment and individual student needs should also be considered.

Which city has the best schools in India?

There is no single city that can be identified as having the best schools for every family. The 2025-26 table includes schools from multiple major education centres, and the most suitable choice depends on factors such as location, curriculum, budget and admission availability.

Are school fees included in the ranking?

Fees are not presented as a single national ranking factor that determines the order of schools. Families should check each school's current fee structure separately because charges vary by institution, grade and academic year.

Should parents rely only on national school rankings?

No. National rankings are useful for research and comparison, but families should also consider local accessibility, curriculum, fees, admissions, facilities and the student's individual requirements.

Final Thoughts

The Top 100 Co-ed Day Schools in India for 2025-26 provide a useful starting point for exploring some of the country's highly regarded day schools. The table brings together schools from different cities and educational environments, making it easier to begin a national comparison.

At the same time, a ranking should be viewed in context. The official rank represents the methodology and survey used for that particular edition. For families choosing a day school, practical factors such as location, curriculum, fees, admission availability and the school's learning environment can be just as important.

The best use of this list is to create a shortlist, investigate each school further and then make a decision based on current information and the student's needs.
