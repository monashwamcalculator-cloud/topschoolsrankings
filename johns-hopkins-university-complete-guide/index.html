What is Johns Hopkins University?

America's First Research University

Johns Hopkins University, founded in 1876, holds the prestigious title of being America's first research university. Located in Baltimore, Maryland, this privately controlled institution transformed the American education system by introducing the German university model, which emphasised specialised training and groundbreaking research.

The university takes its name from Johns Hopkins, a 19th-century Maryland philanthropist and entrepreneur with Quaker roots who believed intensely in improving public health and education. In his will, he set aside $7 million (a massive amount at that time) to establish a hospital, training colleges, an orphanage, and the university itself.

Mission That Still Guides Today

Daniel Coit Gilman, Johns Hopkins' first president, described the university's mission at his 1876 inauguration:

"To educate its students and cultivate their capacity for lifelong learning, to foster independent and original research, and to bring the benefits of discovery to the world."

This mission statement, "Knowledge for the world," remains the university's guiding principle nearly 150 years later.

Research Leadership

What truly distingu Johns Hopkins is its research dominance:

  • Leading recipient of research funding from the federal government every year since 1979

  • In fiscal year 2024, every dollar in federal research investment by the National Institutes of Health generated approximately $2.56 in economic activity

  • Pioneer of historic discoveries including: water purification, CPR, corrective surgery for infant heart defects, and asteroid deflection techniques

  • Championed lifesaving public health interventions like vitamin A supplementation and seat belt safety

  • Authenticated the Dead Sea Scrolls

Admissions Requirements & Process

Acceptance Rate & Competitiveness

Johns Hopkins University is extremely selective:

  • Acceptance rate: 6.43% (one of the lowest in the USA)

  • Graduation rate: 95% (exceptionally high)

  • Classified as a medium, 4-year, private school

This makes Johns Hopkins among the most competitive universities globally, requiring outstanding academic credentials.

Application Deadlines

For undergraduate admission to the School of Arts and Sciences and/or School of Engineering:

 
Decision Plan Deadline
Early Decision I November 1 
Early Decision II January 2 
Regular Decision January 2 
Transfer Applicants March 1 

Applications are accepted for the fall semester from August through January.

  • Two teacher evaluations

  • Mid-year report (due mid-February)

From applicant portal (international students only):

  • International Certification of Finances form

  • Additional optional materials

Standardised Testing

Johns Hopkins considers:

  • SAT: Considered

  • ACT: Considered

  • TOEFL: Considered (for international students)

  • IELTS: Considered (for international students)

International Students Guide

Application Process for International Students

International students follow the same application process but with additional requirements:

  • Application deadline for Fall: January 2

  • No separate application form required

  • No conditional admission offered

  • Early decision options available only

English Language Requirements

For students whose undergraduate instruction was primarily in a language other than English:

  • TOEFL: Required and considered

  • IELTS: Considered

Special Requirements for Foreign University Preparation

Applicants from foreign universities (most cases):

  • Must supplement preparation with one year or more at an accredited US university

  • Exception: Canadian bachelor's degree holders do NOT need this supplement

Financial Requirements for International Students

Students who are neither US citizens nor permanent residents must satisfy the international medical student financial requirement:

  • First-year medical studies: $76,397 USD (non-negotiable)

  • Satisfied through:

    • Cash

    • Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LOC)

    • Combination of cash and LOC

    • Letter of Guarantee (LOG) from embassy/government

Visa Information

  • F1/J1 visa status required

  • International students cannot waive health insurance coverage

  • International Certification of Finances form required

FAQs

 

What is Johns Hopkins University known for?

Johns Hopkins University is known for being America's first research university (founded 1876), receiving the most federal research funding annually since 1979, and pioneering groundbreaking discoveries including CPR, water purification, infant heart surgery, and asteroid deflection techniques. It ranks #7 nationally and #24 globally.

What is the acceptance rate at Johns Hopkins University?

Johns Hopkins University has an acceptance rate of 6.43%, making it one of the most selective universities in the USA. However, it boasts an exceptional 95% graduation rate, indicating that admitted students successfully complete their programmes.

How much does Johns Hopkins University cost in 2026?

For the 2026-27 academic year, Johns Hopkins tuition is $68,670. The total cost of attendance for on-campus freshmen is $94,858, including housing ($13,073), meals ($8,894), books ($1,377), and personal expenses ($1,606).

What are the application deadlines for Johns Hopkins University?

Johns Hopkins undergraduate application deadlines are: Early Decision I on November 1, Early Decision II and Regular Decision on January 2, and Transfer applicants on March 1. Applications are accepted from August through January.

Can international students apply to Johns Hopkins University?

Yes, international students can apply to Johns Hopkins University. They must submit TOEFL or IELTS scores, may need one year of US university preparation (unless they have a Canadian bachelor's degree), and must satisfy financial requirements of $76,397 USD for medical studies.

What programs does Johns Hopkins University offer?

Johns Hopkins offers over 180 programmes across 10 academic divisions, including the School of Arts and Sciences, Whiting School of Engineering, School of Medicine ($66,580 tuition), School of Nursing, School of Education, and School of Public Health. Degrees include certificates, bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programmes.

Is Johns Hopkins University ranked globally?

Yes, Johns Hopkins University ranks #24 in the QS World University Ranking 2026, #16 in Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026, and #18 globally out of 21,462 universities according to CWUR 2025. It consistently ranks among the world's top 20 universities.
