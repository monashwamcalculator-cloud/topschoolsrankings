Editorial Policy

How Top Schools Rankings researches, writes, and updates school and university guides, rankings, and listings.

Last reviewed 20 August 2026

This Editorial Policy explains how Top Schools Rankings creates and maintains school and university content. Our goal is to help families make informed decisions with accurate, readable, and independently researched information.

What we publish

  • Long-form admissions and rankings guides for schools and universities
  • Country and category archives for editorial topics
  • Verified institution listings submitted by schools or our editorial team
  • Member reviews on listing pages

Research standards

Writers use primary sources wherever possible, including official institution websites, government education agencies, accreditation bodies, and established ranking publishers. We cite external sources in articles when statistics, deadlines, or fees are mentioned.

Rankings & lists

Rankings and “top school” lists on this site are informational . They are not official endorsements and do not guarantee admission, employment, or outcomes. Methodology varies by article and is explained in the guide itself. We do not sell placement in editorial rankings.

Original content

All articles must be original work. We do not publish scraped, spun, or duplicate content. AI-assisted drafts, if used, are heavily edited, fact-checked, and reviewed before publication.

Corrections

If you spot an error, email topschoolsrankings@gmail.com with the URL, correction, and an official source link. We aim to review credible correction requests within 7 business days.

Advertising & independence

We may display advertising, including Google AdSense, on some pages. Advertising does not influence editorial rankings or listing verification decisions. Sponsored content, if ever published, will be clearly labeled.

Author transparency

Blog articles include an author bio with contact options. Listing pages show submitter or institution details where available.

Last updated: July 2026.

Our minimum publication standard

  • A clear purpose and intended reader
  • Primary sources for material factual claims
  • Dates and limitations where they affect interpretation
  • No paid ranking positions or fabricated reviews
  • A visible route to request corrections
