<h2>FAQs: UK boarding schools for international students</h2>
<h3>Is Eton always #1 in exam tables?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> No. Parent Power 2026 academic leaders included day-heavy schools such as St Paul’s. Eton remains top-tier for reputation and destinations.</p>
<h3>What is Parent Power?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> The Sunday Times annual guide ranking UK schools largely on GCSE/A-level outcomes with editorial context.</p>
<h3>Do UK boarding schools accept international students?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Yes, many do—subject to places, English level, and visa rules.</p>
<h3>IB or A-level?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> IB suits broad curricula (Sevenoaks is famous); A-levels suit early specialisation for UK universities.</p>
<h3>How early should we register?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> For 13+ entry, families often register years ahead; Sixth Form (16+) timelines are shorter but still competitive.</p>
<h3>Are girls' boarding schools included?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Yes—Cheltenham Ladies', Wycombe Abbey, and Benenden are frequent international shortlist schools.</p>
<h3>Can we use UK boarding as a path to US universities?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Many leavers apply successfully to US colleges; confirm counselling strength at each school.</p>
