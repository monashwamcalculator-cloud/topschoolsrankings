Glenunga IGNITE Program Assessment: Key Dates, ACER HAST & Registration Guide

If your child is aiming for a place in the IGNITE Program at Glenunga International High School (GIHS), understanding the assessment process and the ACER HAST format is the first step toward success.

The IGNITE Program is South Australia’s premier selective pathway for gifted students. Because it offers an accelerated curriculum (completing Years 8–10 in a shorter timeframe), it is highly competitive, drawing hundreds of applicants from across Adelaide for very limited spots.

What Is the Glenunga IGNITE Program?

IGNITE (Intellectual Guided Nurture Individual Talent Education) is a specialized stream for students with high intellectual potential.

  • Accelerated Learning: Students move through core subjects faster than the standard Australian Curriculum.
  • Peer Environment: Your child will study alongside like-minded, high-achieving peers, fostering a culture of academic excellence.
  • International Baccalaureate (IB): As an IB World School, Glenunga integrates global thinking into the IGNITE experience.

Official Assessment Timeline

Unlike many other selective schools, the IGNITE assessment happens early in the school year. Parents must be proactive to avoid missing the deadline.

Note: The test is held while the student is in Year 6 for entry into the program in Year 7. Missing the February deadline usually means waiting another full year or missing the opportunity entirely.

Understanding the ACER HAST Test

Entry is determined by the Higher Ability Selection Test (HAST), managed by ACER. It does not test classroom memory; it tests raw cognitive ability.

  1. Reading Comprehension (45 Mins): Analyzes the ability to infer meaning from complex texts and poetry.
  2. Mathematical Reasoning (40 Mins): Focuses on logic, data interpretation, and pattern-based math (no calculators allowed).
  3. Abstract Reasoning (30 Mins): A non-verbal test using shapes and sequences to measure fluid intelligence.
  4. Written Expression (25 Mins): Students must create a structured, original response to a thematic prompt.

Preparation Strategy for Parents

Since the test targets "Higher Order Thinking," traditional tutoring often fails. Instead, focus on:

  • Timed Practice: The HAST is a high-pressure, fast-paced exam. Accuracy under time constraints is vital.
  • Critical Reading: Encourage reading beyond school level—think scientific articles or classic novels to build a sophisticated vocabulary.
  • Logical Puzzles: Practice non-verbal reasoning puzzles to get comfortable with the Abstract Reasoning section.

Registration Process

  1. Official Portal: Register via the ACER IGNITE website specifically for Glenunga International High School.
  2. Documentation: Be ready to provide NAPLAN results or previous school reports if requested.
  3. Fees: A registration fee is required to secure a seat for the HAST exam.

FAQs

  • Can my child sit the test twice? Generally, students only get one chance at the Year 7 entry level.
  • Is Glenunga the only IGNITE school? No, but it is the most competitive. The Heights School and Aberfoyle Park High School also offer IGNITE streams.
  • What is a "passing" score? There is no fixed pass mark; offers are made to the top-ranking students based on available spots.
