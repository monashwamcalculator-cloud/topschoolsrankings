Oxford vs Cambridge: UK University Comparison Guide (2026)

Oxford vs Cambridge — admissions, tuition, colleges, courses, and international student tips for the UK's top two universities.

Tables, images and URL checked 20 August 2026 · Verify time-sensitive facts
Accuracy note: The original article structure, semantic table rows and useful editorial images have been restored. Admissions, fees, rankings and policies change; follow cited official sources and recheck the date before acting.

University of Oxford and University of Cambridge form the historic core of UK elite higher education. Both use a collegiate system, tutorial/supervision teaching, and highly selective admissions — yet course structures and city environments differ.

FactorOxfordCambridge
LocationOxfordCambridge
Teaching styleTutorials + lecturesSupervisions + lectures
Course structureMany single-honours degrees from year 1Some broader first-year options (e.g. Natural Sciences)

How to decide

Visit both cities if possible. Compare your exact course syllabus, college accommodation, and admissions test requirements. STEM applicants should read faculty research strengths in your niche — both universities lead globally but departmental cultures vary.
