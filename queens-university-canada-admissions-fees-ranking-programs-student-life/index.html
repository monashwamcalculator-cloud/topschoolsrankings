If you're planning to study in Canada at a prestigious and well-established institution, Queen’s University is a top choice.

Known for its academic excellence, strong alumni network, and vibrant campus culture, Queen’s attracts high-achieving students from around the world.

This guide covers everything you need to know — admissions, fees, rankings, programs, and career outcomes.

About Queen’s University

The Queen’s University is located in Kingston, a historic and student-friendly city positioned between Toronto and Montreal.

Founded in 1841, it is one of Canada’s oldest and most respected universities and a member of the U15 Group of Canadian Research Universities.

Key Highlights:

  • 25,000+ students
  • Globally recognized degrees
  • Strong alumni network worldwide
  • High academic standards

Queen’s University Ranking

The Queen’s University consistently performs well in global rankings:

  • QS World University Rankings: Top ~200–250 globally
  • Canada Ranking: Typically Top 10–15 universities
  • Strong reputation in Business, Engineering, and Health Sciences

Rankings may vary slightly each year, but Queen’s maintains a strong global reputation.

Why Choose Queen’s University?

1. Academic Excellence

Queen’s is known for:

  • Rigorous curriculum
  • High-quality teaching
  • Strong academic outcomes

2. World-Class Business School

The Smith School of Business at Queen’s University is one of Canada’s most prestigious business schools.

Globally recognized for:

  • Commerce (BCom)
  • MBA programs
  • Leadership development

3. Powerful Alumni Network

Queen’s has one of the strongest alumni networks in Canada, helping students with:

  • Career opportunities
  • Networking
  • Mentorship

4. Strong Community Experience

Unlike large universities, Queen’s offers:

  • Tight-knit student environment
  • High engagement
  • Strong campus traditions

5. Ideal Location in Kingston

Students benefit from:

  • Safe and peaceful environment
  • Lower cost of living than big cities
  • Access to major cities nearby

Queen’s University Fees

Tuition Fees:

  • Arts & Science: CAD $45,000 – $55,000/year
  • Engineering: CAD $50,000 – $65,000/year
  • Commerce (Business): CAD $60,000+ /year

Fees vary by program and may change annually.

Living Costs in Kingston:

  • Accommodation: $800–$1,400/month
  • Food & transport: $400–$700/month

Total yearly cost: CAD $50,000 – $75,000

Admission Requirements

Undergraduate:

  • Academic score: 85–95%+
  • IELTS: 6.5–7.0 overall

Postgraduate:

  • Bachelor’s degree with strong GPA
  • IELTS: 7.0+
  • SOP + Letters of Recommendation

Competitive programs require higher scores.

Acceptance Rate

The Queen’s University does not officially publish an acceptance rate.

Estimated: Moderate and program-dependent

  • Commerce & Engineering: highly competitive
  • Arts programs: relatively higher acceptance

Top Programs at Queen’s University

Popular programs include:

  • Business (Smith School of Business)
  • Engineering
  • Health Sciences
  • Law
  • Arts & Humanities

Queen’s is especially known for business and leadership-focused education.

Student Life at Queen’s

Queen’s offers one of the best student experiences in Canada:

  • 300+ clubs and societies
  • Strong traditions and events
  • Active campus environment

Students often describe it as community-driven and engaging.

Location Advantage: Kingston

The Kingston location provides:

  • Affordable living
  • Safe environment
  • Balanced lifestyle

Career Opportunities After Graduation

Queen’s graduates benefit from:

  • Strong employer reputation
  • Internship opportunities
  • Alumni connections

Canada also offers:

  • Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)
  • Pathway to Permanent Residency (PR)

Queen’s University vs Other Universities

Queen’s stands out for quality + network + experience.

Who Should Choose Queen’s University?

Best for:

  • High academic achievers
  • Students interested in business or leadership
  • Those seeking strong campus life

Not ideal for:

  • Budget-focused students
  • Students wanting big-city campuses

Conclusion

The Queen’s University is one of Canada’s most respected universities, offering academic excellence, strong career outcomes, and a powerful alumni network.

If you want a prestigious degree with strong community support, Queen’s is an excellent choice.

FAQs

Yes, it offers high-quality education, strong career outcomes, and excellent student life.

It is known for business, engineering, and leadership programs.

3. What is the acceptance rate?

4. Is Queen’s University expensive?

Yes, it is among the higher-cost universities in Canada.

It is located in Kingston.

6. Does Queen’s offer scholarships?

Yes, merit-based and need-based scholarships are available.
