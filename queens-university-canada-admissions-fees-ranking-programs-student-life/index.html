Queen’s University Canada: Admissions, Fees, Ranking, Programs & Student Life (2026–27)

Queen's University at Kingston: QS 2027 =179. International fees 2026–27 from CAD $47,324 to $63,424, Fall 2027 deadlines and scholarships — queensu.ca.

Tables, images and URL checked 14 November 2026 · Verify time-sensitive facts

Quick summary: Queen's University at Kingston charges international students CAD $47,324 a year in total for Arts & Science and CAD $63,424 for first-year Commerce in 2026–27, sits at =179 in the QS World University Rankings 2027, and publishes the grades it looks for — Commerce 90%+, Health Sciences mid-90s, Computing high-70s. Fall 2027 applications close 1 February 2027 for Commerce, Health Sciences and Nursing, and 1 March 2027 for everything else.

Queen's University at Kingston is one of Canada's oldest universities — founded in 1841, a member of the U15 group of research universities, on a compact limestone campus in Kingston, Ontario, between Toronto and Montreal. It is a public university with 28,561 full-time students, including 21,193 undergraduates. In 2025 it received 59,533 applications for 5,477 first-year places; the incoming class averaged 90.5%.

That combination — old, selective, and small-city — is the whole pitch. This guide works through what Queen's itself publishes: the 2026–27 fees, competitive averages, Fall 2027 deadlines, English requirements, scholarships, and residence. Where Queen's publishes nothing — its acceptance rate — this guide says so instead of guessing.

About Queen's University

Queen's opened in 1841, and its limestone main campus is still a single, walkable site. It teaches across Arts and Science, Smith Engineering, the Smith School of Business, Health Sciences (including Medicine and Nursing), Law, Education, Graduate Studies, and Policy Studies. International students are 7.1% of undergraduates and 28% of graduate students, from 99 countries; 94.3% of 2023 entrants returned for second year — above the U15 average of 91.6%.

A Closer Look: Top 5 programmes at Queen's (2026–27 overview)

1. Smith Commerce (BCom) — Kingston, Ontario

Queen's flagship and its hardest programme to enter: a competitive average of 90% or higher, with at least 80% in English, Calculus and Vectors, and one more Grade 12 mathematics course, plus a mandatory supplementary application. International total: CAD $63,424 for the first two years in 2026–27.

2. Smith Engineering (BASc) — Kingston, Ontario

General first year, with ten disciplines to choose from in February; direct-entry routes exist for Chemical, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Mining, Computer, and Mechatronics and Robotics. The competitive average is low-90s for general first year and most direct-entry routes, mid-80s for Computer Engineering, and mid-90s for Mechatronics and Robotics. Every route needs English, Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, Chemistry and Physics; no supplementary application. International total: CAD $61,868.

3. Health Sciences (BHSc, on campus) — Kingston, Ontario

Queen's most selective programme by published average — mid-90s, with English at 80% or higher, plus Biology, Chemistry and Advanced Functions or Calculus — and grades alone cannot earn an offer: a mandatory supplementary application decides among those who clear the grade bar. The surprise is the price: CAD $38,283 in total for first year.

4. Computing (BComp) — Kingston, Ontario

The most accessible of Queen's competitive programmes by published average — high-70s — the realistic entry point for strong students below the 90s. Entry is to the School of Computing, degree plan chosen after first year; prerequisites are English plus Advanced Functions and Calculus and Vectors. International total: CAD $57,367; no supplementary application; the general 1 March 2027 deadline applies.

5. Arts & Science (BA/BSc) — Kingston, Ontario

The broadest entry route at Queen's and the cheapest for international students: CAD $47,324 in total for 2026–27. The competitive average is mid-80s, with English at Grade 12 level as the core requirement; students choose their major after first year. The same faculty also houses Kinesiology (high-80s), the five science streams (mid-80s), and Music (low-80s).

Tuition fees and the real cost of a year (2026–27)

Queen's charges tuition per unit, so the bill moves if you take more or fewer units than the normal full-time load — but the Registrar publishes a total for the normal load. All amounts below are Canadian dollars for 2026–27; each total includes tuition, the Student Assistance Levy, ancillary fees (including health and dental), and the mandatory University Health Insurance Plan (UHIP) of $948 for a September start. The Board can change fees, with increases normally effective 1 September; 2027–28 rates are not yet published.

Source: Queen's Registrar, Undergraduate Tuition & Fees 2026–27 (queensu.ca). Totals include the Student Assistance Levy, ancillary fees and UHIP ($948, September start).

Domestic students pay far less: an Ontario student in Arts and Science pays $7,771.11 in total, Commerce Years 1–2 cost $18,250.15, and Engineering $13,788.75; fee status is fixed by the citizenship status on your first admission application. Residence for 2026–27 costs $13,100.80 to $20,330.80 for eight months, room and meal plan included. The study permit needs proof of funds covering first-year tuition plus CAD $23,448 in living costs for a single applicant outside Quebec.

Admissions: deadlines, competitive averages and how to apply

Undergraduate applications go through the Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC); international applicants use the OUAC 105 form. The Fall 2027 cycle opened on 1 October 2026.

Source: Queen's 2027 Undergraduate Viewbook and admission dates page (queensu.ca).

What grades Queen's actually asks for

Queen's publishes no acceptance rate or fixed cut-offs — only a competitive-averages table: the minimum average, based on prerequisite courses, needed for admission consideration for September of last year. Averages vary each year and could be higher; treat these as the floor for being considered, not a promise of an offer.

*Minimum average for admission consideration for September of last year, as published by Queen's. Source: Queen's competitive averages page (queensu.ca), re-read 30 October – 2 November 2026; Commerce 90%+ confirmed on the Smith BCom page.

Ontario applicants need the Ontario Secondary School Diploma with six Grade 12 university or university/college courses, including English. For Indian applicants, Queen's recognises the All India Senior School Certificate (CBSE) and the Indian School Certificate (CISCE); predicted or interim Year 12 grades are used for students still in school, final board exams for graduates. Programme floors from Queen's India page: Commerce — English and Mathematics at 80% in Standard XII, plus the supplement; Health Sciences — English at 80% plus Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics; Nursing — English at 75% plus the same sciences; Engineering — English, Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics; Computing — English and Mathematics.

English-language requirements

English is the language of instruction, and applicants who have not lived in an English-primary country or studied full-time in English for the most recent three years must prove proficiency with one of the tests below. Results are valid for two years and must be sent by the testing body, reaching Queen's by 31 March if requested. Students who fall short can be considered for QBridge: Accelerated is an eight-week summer course (entry IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 80); EAP is a longer pathway (entry IELTS 5.5 / TOEFL iBT 58).

Source: Queen's University, English Proficiency Requirements (queensu.ca). Graduate applicants face a higher bar — IELTS 7.0 overall and TOEFL iBT 88.

How to apply, step by step

1. Pick the programme and check its prerequisites — Commerce (90%+) differs greatly from Computing (high-70s). 2. Apply through OUAC (international applicants use OUAC 105; fee CAD $156): by 1 February 2027 for Commerce, Health Sciences or Nursing, by 1 March 2027 for everything else. 3. Write the supplementary application if your programme needs one — Commerce, Health Sciences and Nursing, due 15 February 2027. 4. Send documents and English scores by 31 March 2027, and check your SOLUS To-Do List. 5. Apply for residence by 1 June 2027 — a space is guaranteed if your offer arrives on or before 15 April 2027 (31 May 2027 for international students) and the $805 deposit is in by 1 June at 4 pm ET. 6. Sort the study permit early: proof of funds for first-year tuition plus CAD $23,448 in living costs.

Scholarships for international students

Queen's assesses international applicants for its main awards automatically — no separate application — through the Dean's International Award program, covering first-entry programmes in Arts and Science, Engineering, Commerce and Health Sciences. Awards are renewable for three further years, four in total, if you stay full-time in your original faculty. Canadian citizens, dual citizens and permanent residents do not qualify, nor do international students paying domestic fees.

Source: Queen's University Registrar, financial aid and awards pages (queensu.ca). Maximum Dean's award: $25,000 a year — up to $100,000 over four years.

Two things applicants often get wrong. First, the Principal's Scholarship ($7,000, one year, automatic) goes to students at the top of the competitive admission average in each first-entry programme — it is not something you apply for. Second, the Major Admission Awards, including the Chancellor's Scholarship, are only for Canadian citizens, permanent residents and protected persons — not open to international students at all. The one major international award needing a separate application is the International Chancellor's Scholarship, worth up to $72,000 over four years, applied for through SOLUS.

Student life, residence and Kingston

The Alma Mater Society — Canada's oldest student government — supports more than 300 ratified clubs. Residence is predominantly first-year: about 4,800 spaces are available for Fall 2027, guaranteed if your offer arrives on or before 15 April 2027 (31 May 2027 for international students) and you apply with the $805 deposit by 1 June 2027. Kingston is a historic city on Lake Ontario, roughly midway between Toronto and Montreal, with a campus compact enough that most students live within walking distance. Residence totals $13,100.80 to $20,330.80 for eight months with the meal plan included.

Who should choose Queen's University?

Best for: high achievers aiming at Commerce, Health Sciences or Engineering who can meet the published averages; students who want a close-knit, campus-centred university in a smaller city; applicants who value the automatic Dean's International Awards ($10,000–$25,000 a year). Think twice if: your budget cannot stretch to roughly $47,000–$63,000 a year before living costs, or you want a big-city campus.

Conclusion

Queen's — founded in 1841, ranked =179 in QS 2027 — but its real selling point is how much it publishes: fees on the Registrar's schedule to the dollar, competitive averages by programme, fixed deadlines, automatic scholarships. Read those four things against your own grades and budget: Commerce at 90%+ and $63,424 a year means applying by 1 February 2027; Arts and Science at mid-80s and $47,324 gives you until 1 March. Verify the current figures on queensu.ca before you pay anything — this guide is checked 14 November 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Queen's University cost for international students?

In 2026–27, the total for a full-time international undergraduate — tuition plus the Student Assistance Levy, ancillary fees and mandatory health insurance (UHIP) — is CAD $47,324 for Arts & Science, $57,367 for Computing, $61,868 for Engineering, $63,424 for first- and second-year Commerce, $38,283 for first-year Health Sciences and $56,043 for Nursing. Residence adds $13,101 to $20,331 for eight months, meal plan included.

What grades do I need to get into Queen's University?

Queen's publishes competitive averages — the minimum average, based on prerequisite courses, needed for admission consideration for September of last year. They run from high-70s for Computing, through mid-80s for Arts and the science streams, low-90s for Engineering and Nursing, to 90%+ for Smith Commerce and mid-90s for Health Sciences. Commerce, Health Sciences and Nursing also require a supplementary application.

What is Queen's University's acceptance rate?

Queen's does not publish an institutional acceptance rate, so any percentage quoted elsewhere is an estimate with no official source. The useful published measure is the competitive average by programme above, plus the 2025 context: 59,533 first-year applications for 5,477 places.

What are the application deadlines for Fall 2027?

Applications open through OUAC on 1 October 2026. Commerce, Health Sciences and Nursing close on 1 February 2027, with their mandatory supplementary applications due 15 February 2027. All other first-year programmes close on 1 March 2027, and all supporting documents are due by 31 March 2027. Residence applications and the $805 deposit are due by 1 June 2027 at 4 pm ET.

Does Queen's University offer scholarships to international students?

Yes. International students are automatically considered for the Dean's International Award program — $10,000 to $25,000 a year, renewable for up to four years — with no separate application. The Principal's Scholarship pays $7,000 for one year, also automatic. The Major Admission Awards are only for Canadian citizens, permanent residents and protected persons.
