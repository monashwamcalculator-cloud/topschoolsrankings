<div class="answer-box"><span>Quick answer</span><p>Search the exact provider or course in the Australian Government CRICOS register, then match the course code, provider code, campus, duration and level with your offer and the institution’s official page.</p></div><div class="editor-note"><strong>Editorial note:</strong> Education, admissions, fees and immigration rules can change. Check the linked official source again before applying or paying.</div><section><h2>What CRICOS is</h2><p>CRICOS is the Australian Government’s official register of education providers and courses available to people studying in Australia on student visas. It is the correct starting point for checking whether the provider and course appear in the international-student register.</p><p>A ranking page, agent list or university marketing page is not a substitute for the register. CRICOS also does not promise admission, a visa or employment outcome.</p></section><section><h2>Search using the strongest identifier</h2><p>If the offer or course page shows a CRICOS course code, search that code first. Otherwise search the legal provider name and locate the exact course. Some institutions have multiple locations and codes, so name-only matching is not enough.</p></section><section><h2>Match these fields</h2><p>Compare the register with your offer letter and the institution’s current course page. Ask for written clarification if any field differs before paying a deposit.</p><ul><li>Provider name and provider code</li><li>Course title and CRICOS course code</li><li>Qualification level</li><li>Campus or delivery location</li><li>Registered duration</li><li>Any conditions or notes shown in the record</li></ul></section><section><h2>Add quality and outcome context</h2><p>After the registration check, QILT and ComparED can provide evidence about student experience and graduate outcomes across Australian higher education. QILT explains that its comparison data comes from the Student Experience Survey and Graduate Outcomes Survey.</p><p>Interpret results with the published context. Response rates, subject groupings, institution size and labour markets can affect comparisons. Use the numbers to form questions rather than to guarantee your own outcome.</p></section><section><h2>Check the current offer and costs</h2><p>Confirm the intake, tuition, compulsory fees, refund terms, health-cover requirements and course delivery directly with the provider. Keep visa questions tied to the latest Department of Home Affairs guidance, because registration and visa eligibility are related but separate checks.</p></section><section><h2>When to stop the application</h2><ul><li>The course or provider cannot be located in CRICOS.</li><li>The code belongs to a different provider, course or location.</li><li>The offer omits the code and the provider will not clarify it.</li><li>A third party promises a guaranteed visa or job.</li><li>Payment is requested through a channel the provider does not confirm.</li></ul></section><section class="faq-section"><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><details><summary>Does a CRICOS listing guarantee a student visa?</summary><p>No. CRICOS confirms registration status; the Department of Home Affairs assesses visa applications under current rules.</p></details><details><summary>Why should I match the campus?</summary><p>Providers can operate at multiple locations, and the exact registered course and location should match the offer you are accepting.</p></details><details><summary>Can QILT replace a CRICOS check?</summary><p>No. QILT adds student-experience and outcomes context; CRICOS is the official course and provider register for overseas students.</p></details></section><section class="source-section"><h2>Primary sources</h2><p>Sources were accessed for this guide on 20 August 2026.</p><ul><li><a href="https://cricos.education.gov.au/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Australian Government CRICOS</a></li><li><a href="https://www.qilt.edu.au/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">QILT</a></li><li><a href="https://www.qilt.edu.au/comparED" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">QILT ComparED information</a></li></ul></section>
