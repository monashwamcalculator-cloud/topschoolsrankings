Because it offers an accelerated curriculum (completing Years 8–10 in a shorter timeframe), it is highly competitive, drawing hundreds of applicants from across Adelaide for very limited spots.</p> <h3>What Is the Glenunga IGNITE Program?</h3> <p>IGNITE (Intellectual Guided Nurture Individual Talent Education) is a specialized stream for students with high intellectual potential.</p> <ul> <li><strong>Accelerated Learning:</strong> Students move through core subjects faster than the standard Australian Curriculum.</li> <li><strong>Peer Environment:</strong> Your child will study alongside like-minded, high-achieving peers, fostering a culture of academic excellence.</li> <li><strong>International Baccalaureate (IB):</strong> As an IB World School, Glenunga integrates global thinking into the IGNITE experience.</li> </ul> <h3>Official Assessment Timeline</h3> <p>Unlike many other selective schools, the IGNITE assessment happens <strong>early in the school year</strong>. Parents must be proactive to avoid missing the deadline.</p>
