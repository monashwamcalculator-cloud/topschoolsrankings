About University of Birmingham

The University of Birmingham is a research-intensive public university in Birmingham, England, with university status from 1900 and earlier civic college roots. Its Edgbaston campus hosts a broad range of undergraduate and postgraduate degrees across arts, sciences, social sciences, engineering, medicine-related pathways and business disciplines. Birmingham offers a large-campus experience in one of the UK’s major cities.

Undergraduate applicants usually apply through UCAS and should review course entry requirements and English minima on birmingham.ac.uk. International students must confirm fee status, visa documentation and accommodation timelines. The university publishes open-day and offer-holder event information each cycle.

Campus address commonly cited: Edgbaston, Birmingham B15 2TT, United Kingdom. Confirm tuition, scholarships and living-cost guidance on the official site before applying. UCAS applicants should check course-specific entry grades and English requirements carefully. Confirm accommodation windows soon after receiving an offer.
