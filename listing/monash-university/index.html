About Monash University

Monash University is a public research university established on 30 May 1958 and named after Sir John Monash. Its foundation Clayton campus is located on Wellington Road in Melbourne's south-eastern suburbs, with additional Victorian campuses at Caulfield, Peninsula and Parkville, as well as international campuses and centres.

Monash offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across ten faculties spanning the arts, business and economics, education, engineering, information technology, law, medicine, nursing and health sciences, pharmacy and pharmaceutical sciences, and science. The university is a member of the Group of Eight and is recognised for research in medicine, engineering, law and the social sciences.

Domestic prospective students can call 1800 MONASH (1800 666 274). International prospective students can call +61 3 9903 4788. The university switchboard is available on +61 3 9902 6000, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.
