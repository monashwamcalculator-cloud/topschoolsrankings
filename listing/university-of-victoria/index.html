About University of Victoria

The University of Victoria (UVic) is a public university in Victoria, British Columbia, with university status from 1963 and earlier college roots. Located on Vancouver Island, UVic offers undergraduate and graduate programmes across humanities, science, engineering, business, education, law and fine arts. The campus setting combines a research university environment with a smaller-city coastal location.

Applicants should review faculty requirements, English minima and deadlines on uvic.ca. International students must confirm study-permit steps, fee status and housing options carefully. Co-op education is a notable pathway in many faculties.

Campus address: 3800 Finnerty Road, Victoria, BC V8P 5C2. Confirm tuition, residence applications and scholarship timelines on the official website before applying.
