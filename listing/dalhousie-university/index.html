About Dalhousie University

Dalhousie University is a public university in Halifax, Nova Scotia, founded in 1818. It is one of Atlantic Canada’s major research universities, offering undergraduate and graduate programmes across arts and social sciences, science, engineering, management, health professions and law. Halifax provides a coastal city setting with regional clinical and research connections.

Applicants should review faculty admission requirements, English minima and deadlines on dal.ca. International students must confirm study-permit documentation, fee status and residence options carefully. Some professional programmes have additional prerequisites and competitive entry.

Campus address commonly cited: Halifax, NS (South Street / University Avenue area). Confirm tuition, housing and scholarship timelines on the official website before applying.
