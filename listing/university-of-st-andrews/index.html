About University of St Andrews

The University of St Andrews is Scotland’s oldest university, founded in 1413 in the coastal town of St Andrews, Fife. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate degrees across arts, divinity, medicine, science and related academic schools. The university is known for a close-knit student community, strong international enrolment and a distinctive town-and-gown setting in a compact historic location.

Undergraduate applicants typically apply through UCAS and should review subject-specific entry requirements, English-language minima and deadlines on st-andrews.ac.uk. International students must confirm fee status, visa documentation and any programme-specific conditions. St Andrews publishes detailed guidance for applicants from different qualification systems, including A Levels, IB and international secondary credentials.
