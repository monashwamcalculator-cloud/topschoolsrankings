About University of York

The University of York is a public university in York, North Yorkshire, founded in 1963. It is organised with colleges that support student community life alongside academic departments. York offers undergraduate and postgraduate degrees across arts and humanities, sciences, social sciences and interdisciplinary fields on campuses around Heslington and the city.

Undergraduate applicants typically apply through UCAS and should review course requirements and English minima on york.ac.uk. International students must confirm fee status, visa documentation and accommodation processes carefully. York’s historic city setting is a major part of the student experience.
