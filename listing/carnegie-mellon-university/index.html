About Carnegie Mellon University

Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) is a private research university in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, with roots dating to 1900. It is widely recognised for computer science, engineering, business and fine arts, alongside humanities and social sciences. Undergraduate applicants typically apply to a specific college or school within CMU, and requirements can differ by programme.

Admission is highly selective, especially in computing and engineering pathways. Review college-specific guidance, testing policy and portfolio requirements where relevant on admission.enrollment.cmu.edu. CMU is accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE). International applicants should confirm English requirements and documentation carefully.

Campus address: 5000 Forbes Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15213. Confirm deadlines, tuition, housing and visit programmes on cmu.edu before applying.
