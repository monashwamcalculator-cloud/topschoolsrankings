About Milton Academy

Milton Academy is an independent school in Milton, Massachusetts, with institutional roots dating to 1798. Its Upper School offers a college-preparatory curriculum with strong arts and athletics programmes, and boarding options for students who live on campus. The location south of Boston provides access to the wider Boston academic and cultural region.

Admission is selective. Families should review Upper School application requirements, boarding availability and financial-aid timelines on milton.edu. Confirm current day and boarding tuition on the official fee publications. Campus visits help families understand the mix of day and boarding student life.

Campus address: 170 Centre Street, Milton, MA 02186. Confirm admissions contacts and deadlines on the official website before applying.
