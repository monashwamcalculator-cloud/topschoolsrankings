About Phillips Academy Andover

Phillips Academy Andover is an independent, coeducational boarding and day school founded in 1778 in Andover, Massachusetts, about 20 miles north of Boston.

Andover offers a broad college-preparatory program for grades 9 through 12 and post-graduates, with need-blind admission and financial aid meeting 100% of demonstrated need for admitted students. Students participate in a wide range of academic, artistic, and athletic opportunities on a residential campus organized into student clusters.

The Office of Admission welcomes inquiries at admissions@andover.edu or 978-749-4050. Campus visits and virtual events are available through andover.edu.
