About Scotch College Melbourne

Scotch College Melbourne is an independent Presbyterian boys' day and boarding school founded in 1851. Its single 27-hectare campus at 1 Morrison Street in Hawthorn sits beside the Yarra River in Melbourne's inner east, educating boys from Prep through Year 12 with boarders accommodated from Years 7 to 12.

The school offers a broad curriculum culminating in the Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE). Christian teachings underpin school life, and students participate in extensive co-curricular programmes spanning music, sport, outdoor education, leadership and service. Scotch is affiliated with the Presbyterian Church of Victoria and is a member of the Associated Public Schools of Victoria.

Applications for enrolment are made online through the school website. The main reception can be reached on +61 3 9810 4321. Scotch College holds VRQA registration and is registered on CRICOS (provider code 00624A) for international students.
