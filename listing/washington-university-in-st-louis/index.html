About Washington University in St. Louis

Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) is a private research university founded in 1853. Based primarily in St. Louis, Missouri, it offers selective undergraduate programmes in arts and sciences, engineering, business and art, alongside graduate and professional schools including medicine and law. WashU is known for research intensity and a residential undergraduate experience on the Danforth Campus.

Undergraduate admission is highly selective. Applicants should review school-specific options, testing policy and financial-aid information on admissions.wustl.edu. WashU is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC). International students must confirm English-language and documentation requirements.

Campus address commonly cited: 1 Brookings Drive, St. Louis, MO 63130. Confirm tuition, housing, deadlines and visit programmes on wustl.edu before applying.
