About University of Technology Sydney

The University of Technology Sydney (UTS) is a public Australian university in Ultimo, central Sydney, with university status from 1988 and earlier institute roots. UTS is known for practice-oriented teaching across business, design, engineering, information technology, health, law, science and communication. Its city-campus location supports industry connections across Sydney.

Domestic applicants commonly use UAC pathways; international applicants apply through UTS international admissions. Confirm English requirements, selection ranks and CRICOS course codes on uts.edu.au. International students must verify visa and OSHC requirements for their intake.

Campus address commonly cited: 15 Broadway, Ultimo NSW 2007. Confirm tuition, scholarships, housing and application deadlines on the official website before applying.
