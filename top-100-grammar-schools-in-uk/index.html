Top 100 grammar schools in the UK: What This Ranking Means

This page is a research guide, not an official government endorsement and not a promise of admission or outcomes. “Top” means the schools performed strongly against the disclosed editorial criteria or appear in the identified source order. It does not mean that a one-place difference is educationally significant, that every school accepts the same applicants, or that the first school is the right choice for every child.

The table is designed for entity-level comparison: school name, location, sector or format, curriculum and boarding information are stated directly where the topic requires them. That structure helps families, search engines and AI answer systems distinguish schools with similar names without relying on vague prose.

How We Ranked the Top 100 grammar schools in the UK

Parent Power is a publisher ranking rather than a government league table. We preserve the order reported in the current accessible grammar-school table, identify the relevant nation and use official DfE data only where definitions are comparable. DfE states that Progress 8 cannot be calculated for 2024/25 or 2025/26 because the relevant cohorts lack KS2 prior-attainment data, so no current Progress 8 figure is used in this order. Results can reflect highly selective intake, so rank is not a causal measure of teaching quality.

What the method does not do

  • It does not sell or reserve ranking positions.
  • It does not treat one exam average as a complete measure of school quality.
  • It does not assume selective and open-admissions schools have comparable intakes.
  • It does not reproduce an outdated table without naming the source edition and check date.
  • It does not replace current admissions, fee, safeguarding or programme verification.

Key Findings From the Top 100 grammar schools in the UK

  1. London and the South East are highly visible near the top, but strong selective-school clusters also appear in Buckinghamshire, Kent, Essex, Birmingham, Gloucestershire and Northern Ireland.
  2. A selective admissions test changes the meaning of outcomes. DfE cannot calculate Progress 8 for 2024/25 or 2025/26, so treat older progress figures as historical and compare current destinations, subject access and pastoral indicators alongside headline grades.
  3. Grammar-school eligibility can depend on residence, catchment, pupil-premium priority, faith criteria or a score threshold. A high test score does not always guarantee a place.

11-Plus Admissions for Top Grammar Schools in the UK

There is no single UK 11-plus. Areas and individual schools can use different providers, combinations of verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, mathematics and English, and different standardisation or age-adjustment rules. Registration often closes well before the ordinary secondary-school application deadline.

Create an admissions sheet for each target school with six fields: test provider, registration deadline, test date, qualifying standard, oversubscription order and catchment or priority-area rule. Read the current policy PDF from the admission authority. A third-party summary is useful for orientation but should never decide where a family lives or whether a child is eligible.

Grammar-school results and selection effects

Strong attainment is expected in a cohort selected for prior academic performance. DfE cannot calculate Progress 8 for 2024/25 or 2025/26 because the cohorts lack KS2 prior-attainment data. Use older progress figures only as historical context, then compare current destinations, curriculum breadth, sixth-form retention and outcomes for disadvantaged pupils. Inspection findings and pastoral evidence answer questions that an exam table cannot.

How to Choose From the Top 100 grammar schools in the UK

  • Read the current admissions policy from the school or admission authority; never rely on a tutoring website's summary.
  • Check the exact test provider, question types, registration deadline, exam date and catchment rules.
  • Compare Attainment 8, sixth-form subjects, destinations, SEND support and travel time; note that current Progress 8 is unavailable for 2024/25 and 2025/26.
  • Use open events to test whether the pace, pastoral model and extracurricular offer suit the child.

A five-step family decision process

  1. Set non-negotiables: eligible grade, location, curriculum, language, day or boarding, support and maximum all-in cost.
  2. Check access: catchment, passport, test, interview, prerequisite, visa and deadline rules.
  3. Verify evidence: use official or accreditor sources and record the date, cohort and definition behind every statistic.
  4. Test daily fit: model travel, timetable, workload, residence life, extracurricular access and student support.
  5. Compare offers: evaluate the written place, conditions, total cost, aid, refund terms and transition plan—not the ranking number alone.

FAQs About the Top 100 grammar schools in the UK

What is the best grammar school in the UK?

Queen Elizabeth's School, Barnet is first in the Parent Power 2026 grammar-school table used for this guide. Other publishers can use different measures and produce a different order.

Are grammar schools free?

Most grammar schools in England and Northern Ireland are state-funded and do not charge tuition, although families may still face transport, uniform, meals and activity costs.

Do all UK nations have grammar schools?

No. The large modern selective sectors are in England and Northern Ireland. Scotland and Wales do not have a directly comparable national grammar-school system.

Is the 11-plus the same everywhere?

No. Test providers, subjects, standardisation, qualifying scores and priority rules vary by area and school.

Does passing the 11-plus guarantee admission?

Not necessarily. Oversubscription, catchment, distance and priority criteria can still determine who receives an offer.

Are grammar-school rankings official?

No. The UK government publishes school performance data, but a publisher decides how to combine measures into an overall ranking.

What data should parents compare beyond GCSE grades?

Progress measures, sixth-form results, destinations, curriculum breadth, attendance, inspection evidence, support provision and admissions accessibility.

How current is this top 100 grammar schools list?

The source table and official context were checked on August 24, 2026. Admissions rules should be rechecked for the child's actual entry year.
