How It Works

Learn how Top Schools Rankings works — browse verified school listings, write reviews, list your institution, or claim an existing profile.

Last reviewed 20 August 2026

Top Schools Rankings connects families with schools and universities through a verified directory , editorial guides, and community reviews. Here is how each part of the platform works.

For Parents & Students

Browse the schools & universities directory . Filter by category, country, city, or search by name. Open any listing to see contact details, photos, website link, and parent reviews.

Browse listings

Create a free account with email or Google . Sign in and leave one honest review per listing. You cannot review your own institution.

Create account

List Your School or University

Sign in and submit the short listing form — institution details, website, contact info, logo, and cover image. Complete listings go live instantly.

Add your listing

Claim an Existing Listing

If your school is already listed, sign in and use Claim this listing on the profile page to manage details from your account.

Keep Your Verified Badge

Verified listings must be updated at least every 60 days . You will receive an email reminder 7 days before expiry. Update contact details, photos, or descriptions from your account dashboard.

Read Our Guides

Explore admissions guides, rankings, and country-specific articles on our blog . Writers can contribute via Write for Us .

Listing Requirements

  • Institution name, type (School / College / University / Institute), and year established
  • Country, state, city, and address
  • Website, email, and phone number
  • About your institution (150–300 words) and courses offered
  • Logo and cover image (JPG/PNG/WEBP, max 200 KB each)
  • Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn — optional
  • One listing per institution (duplicate prevention by website)
