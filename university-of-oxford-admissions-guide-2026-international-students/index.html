University of Oxford Admissions 2026: Acceptance Rates, Fees & International Student Guide

Complete 2026 admissions guide for international students applying to the University of Oxford: UCAS deadlines (Oct 15), entrance tests, acceptance rates, tuition fees, and college selection.

Tables, images and URL checked 20 August 2026 · Verify time-sensitive facts

University of Oxford admissions represent one of the premier undergraduate entry processes globally. Ranked #4 worldwide in the QS World University Rankings 2026 (and consistently inside the top 5 of major global tables as detailed in our Top 10 UK Universities for International Students guide), Oxford evaluates applicants through a multi-stage process combining academic performance, subject-specific entrance tests, written work, and rigorous academic interviews.

This 2026 guide breaks down everything international families need to know: key UCAS deadlines, realistic acceptance rates, international tuition fee bands, collegiate choices, and test registration steps—grounded in official guidance from Oxford Undergraduate Admissions. 1. The Oxford Application Timeline (Key Dates)

Unlike standard UK university timelines that extend into January, Oxford operates on a strict early deadline schedule. International applicants must adhere to the following key milestones:

  • June – September: Course research, college selection, and subject test preparation.
  • September 1: UCAS application portal opens for submission.
  • September 29: Final deadline to register for Oxford subject admissions tests (MAT, PAT, TSA, etc.).
  • October 15 (18:00 UK Time): Strict UCAS submission deadline for Oxford applications.
  • Late October – Early November: Sit required entrance tests at authorized test centers worldwide.
  • November 10: Deadline for submitting written work samples (if required by your course).
  • December: Shortlisted candidates attend online academic interviews conducted by Oxford college tutors.
  • Mid-January 2026: Final decision letters released to all applicants via UCAS Track.

2. Entry Requirements for International Applicants

Oxford accepts secondary school qualifications from across the world, but requirements are demanding. Offers are conditional based on final year examination results:

A-Levels and International Baccalaureate (IB)

  • A-Levels: Standard offers range between AAA for Humanities (e.g., History, English) and A*A*A for Science & Mathematics (e.g., Mathematics, Computer Science, Engineering Science).
  • IB Diploma: Total score of 38 to 40 points, including 6 or 7 in subjects specified at Higher Level (HL).

US Qualifications (Advanced Placement & SAT)

Applicants with US High School Diplomas must present advanced subject testing:

  • AP Courses: 4 or 5 Grade 5 AP scores in relevant subjects, combined with high GPA.
  • SAT / ACT: Optional or supplementary; subject-specific AP scores carry primary weight.

English Language Proficiency

Non-native English speakers must meet either Oxford's Standard or Higher level requirement:

  • Standard Level (select courses): IELTS Academic 7.0 (minimum 6.5 per component) or TOEFL iBT 100.
  • Higher Level (most courses): IELTS Academic 7.5 (minimum 7.0 per component) or TOEFL iBT 110.

3. Understanding Oxford Colleges & Open Applications

Oxford is a collegiate university comprising over 30 independent colleges (such as Christ Church, Balliol, Magdalen, and Trinity). Every undergraduate belongs both to the central University and to a specific college.

When filling out your UCAS form, you have two choices:

  1. Specify a Preference College: Choose a specific college based on location, accommodation, size, or historical preference.
  2. Make an Open Application: UCAS automatically allocates your application to a college that has received relatively fewer applications for your course in that admissions cycle.

Important Note: Oxford uses a pooling mechanism during interviews to ensure that qualified candidates receive places regardless of which college they initially applied to.

4. Oxford International Tuition Fees & Cost of Living (2026)

Tuition fees for international (non-UK) students are divided into four main course fee bands:

Course Fee Band Example Subjects Estimated Annual International Tuition
Band 1 (Humanities & Social Sciences) History, Classics, Philosophy, English, Law £33,050 – £37,500 / year
Band 2 (Mathematics & Applied Sciences) Mathematics, Statistics, Economics & Management £38,000 – £43,500 / year
Band 3 (Lab-Based Sciences & Engineering) Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Engineering £45,000 – £52,000 / year
Band 4 (Clinical Medicine) Medicine (Clinical Years 4–6) £58,600 / year

In addition to tuition, the University estimates living expenses in Oxford for 2026/27 to range between £1,345 and £1,955 per month to cover accommodation, food, study materials, and personal expenses over a 9-month academic session.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the UCAS deadline for Oxford University 2026 entry?

The deadline for October 2026 entry is October 15, 2025 at 18:00 UK time. Late applications are not considered by Oxford.

Can I apply to both Oxford and Cambridge in the same year?

No. Under UCAS rules, undergraduate applicants can apply to either Oxford or Cambridge in the same application cycle, not both.

What is the acceptance rate for international students at Oxford?

The overall undergraduate acceptance rate at Oxford is around 13.5%–16.5%. However, international acceptance rates vary significantly by course, ranging from ~5% for Computer Science or Medicine to ~20% for Classics.

Are international students eligible for financial aid at Oxford?

Oxford offers a limited number of competitive international scholarships (such as Reach Oxford Scholarships and Simon and June Li Scholarships). However, financial support is highly competitive, and most international students self-fund their studies.
