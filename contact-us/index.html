Contact Us

Contact TopSchoolsRankings for school & university corrections, guest posts, topic ideas and general questions. Editorial replies within 3-7 business days.

Last reviewed 20 August 2026

Contact TopSchoolsRankings.com

Thank you for visiting TopSchoolsRankings.com . We welcome questions, corrections, and feedback about our school and university guides, rankings, and articles. Reach our editorial team at topschoolsrankings@gmail.com —we read every genuine message and prioritize accuracy and clarity.

At a glance

  • We cover: K–12 schools and universities (rankings, comparisons, admissions guides)
  • Typical reply time: 3–7 business days (corrections often sooner)
  • Guest posts: Guidelines on our Write for Us page
  • About the site: See About Us

Why Contact Us

1. Content corrections (schools or universities)

If a fee, date, program name, ranking detail, or fact is wrong or outdated, send the article URL , what should change, and a link to an official source (institution site, regulator, or board) when possible. We update verified corrections responsibly.

2. Institution information updates

School or university representatives may submit factual updates for review (contact details, accreditation, publicly listed programs). Submission does not guarantee a new article, ranking placement, or promotional coverage. Editorial standards and independence always apply.

3. Guest post & writer inquiries

Pitch school- or university-focused articles with subject line Guest Post Pitch – [Topic] . Include outline, author bio, and samples as described on Write for Us. General “SEO guest post” spam is not answered.

4. General questions & reader feedback

Questions about rankings, coverage, or topic ideas (e.g., best universities in a region, CBSE school comparisons) are welcome. We cannot fulfill every request but value constructive feedback.

What We Do Not Provide

TopSchoolsRankings.com does not offer admission consulting, guaranteed placement, ranking positions for payment, or dispute mediation between families and institutions. For admissions, fees, scholarships, visas, or enrollment, contact the institution directly or an accredited advisor.

How to Write Your Email

Use a clear subject line, for example:

  • Correction – [Article Title]
  • School/University Update – [Institution Name]
  • Guest Post Pitch – [Topic]
  • General Inquiry – [Short Topic]

Include your name and role, the full page URL (if applicable), a clear description, and supporting links for factual claims. Incomplete messages may not receive a reply.

Response Times & Communication

We aim to respond within 3–7 business days . Factual corrections are prioritized over promotional mail. Please keep messages professional; spam, bulk link-building, threats, or abusive language may be ignored.

Privacy & Policies

Email details are used only to handle your request; we do not sell personal data. Privacy, disclaimer, and terms on this site apply to all communications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I request a new ranking article for my city or country?

Yes—suggest a region and school or university focus. We cannot promise timelines or publication.

Do you respond to partnership or advertising emails?

Clearly labeled proposals may be reviewed; response is not guaranteed. Any sponsored content would be disclosed to readers.

How do I report a copyright or content concern?

Send the page URL, your relationship to the material, and specifics so we can review under our site terms.

Before You Go

TopSchoolsRankings.com grows through accurate, independent school and university research. Your corrections and feedback help us stay trustworthy—we appreciate you reaching out.
