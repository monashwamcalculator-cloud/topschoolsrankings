Top 10 STEM Boarding Schools in the USA (2026 Guide)

The top 10 STEM boarding schools in the USA for 2026, ranked by science research programs, math competition results, and robotics strength - not just overall Niche rank.

Overview: Most “best boarding school” lists rank by overall Niche score or general reputation. This guide takes a different angle: the top 10 STEM boarding schools in the USA, selected specifically for science research facilities, math competition depth (AMC/AIME/USAMO), robotics programs, and computer science course offerings. All ten are elite New England or California boarding schools with residential science research options — not day-only STEM academies.

Parents searching “best STEM boarding schools” often land on generic prep-school rankings that weigh sports, arts, and college matriculation equally with science and math. This list narrows the focus: which residential boarding schools give a serious STEM-track student the strongest labs, research mentorship, competitive math culture, and computer science depth before college?

How we selected these STEM boarding schools

  • Dedicated science research programs: Schools offering independent or mentored research beyond the standard AP curriculum
  • Math competition culture: Active participation in AMC, AIME, and (for top performers) USAMO, plus math team depth
  • Computer science & robotics: Standalone CS departments, robotics teams, and hackathon/competition participation
  • Facilities: Purpose-built science centers, maker spaces, and specialized labs
  • Boarding format: All ten are traditional residential boarding schools, not day-only STEM magnet schools

Choate’s Kohler Environmental Center: a model STEM program

Choate Rosemary Hall’s Kohler Environmental Center (KEC) is one of the most distinctive STEM offerings among elite boarding schools: a residential, semester-long environmental science immersion where a small cohort of students lives and studies at an off-campus, sustainability-focused facility, combining fieldwork, data collection, and interdisciplinary coursework. Programs like this illustrate how STEM strength at the boarding-school level increasingly means hands-on, research-format learning — not just AP course counts.

Math competitions: a useful, verifiable STEM signal

Because private schools do not always publish detailed STEM outcome data, one of the more objective signals families can check is participation in the American Mathematics Competitions (AMC/AIME), run by the Mathematical Association of America (MAA). Schools with strong STEM cultures typically:

  • Register large cohorts for AMC 10/12 each year, not just a handful of top students
  • Produce multiple AIME qualifiers annually
  • Occasionally produce USAMO qualifiers or Math Olympiad program invitees, an extremely high bar nationally

Ask an admissions office directly for recent competition results — official participation data is published by the MAA but is not always broken out publicly by individual school.

Summer research programs that complement these schools

How to evaluate STEM strength during your school search

  1. Request the science and CS course catalog, not just the general curriculum guide.
  2. Ask about independent research or thesis options and how many students complete one per year.
  3. Check math team participation numbers and recent AIME/USAMO results.
  4. Tour the science facilities in person or virtually — age and equipment quality vary widely even among elite schools.
  5. Ask about university partnerships or access to nearby research labs and summer programs.

Frequently asked questions: top STEM boarding schools in USA

Which boarding school has the best STEM program in the USA?

There is no single official “STEM ranking” for boarding schools, but Phillips Exeter Academy, Phillips Academy (Andover), and Choate Rosemary Hall (via its Kohler Environmental Center) are consistently cited for the depth of their science, math, and research offerings among elite Northeastern boarding schools.

Do boarding schools offer real scientific research opportunities, or just AP classes?

The strongest STEM boarding schools go beyond AP coursework, offering independent research seminars, mentored lab projects, and in some cases residential immersion programs (like Choate’s Kohler Environmental Center). Always ask a specific school what percentage of students complete an independent research project.

Are these STEM boarding schools also academically selective overall?

Yes — all ten schools on this list are highly selective, elite boarding schools with competitive admissions across all subjects, not STEM-only admission tracks.

Do STEM-strong boarding schools have good computer science programs?

Most do, though depth varies. Schools like Loomis Chaffee and Deerfield have expanded dedicated CS departments and data science courses in recent years; ask for the current CS course sequence when researching, since offerings change year to year.

Is there a formal ranking of STEM boarding schools like there is for colleges?

No. Unlike U.S. News college rankings, there is no standardized, numerically ranked list of STEM boarding school programs. This guide is based on program depth, facilities, and competition participation rather than a single official methodology.

How much does it cost to attend a top STEM boarding school?

Full boarding tuition, room, and board typically runs $65,000–$78,000 per year at these schools. Several offer generous need-based financial aid, including no-loan policies at some institutions — confirm current figures directly with each admissions office.

Can international students study at these STEM boarding schools?

Yes, all ten schools enroll international boarding students, typically on an F-1 student visa. International admission is highly competitive and often need-aware for financial aid purposes — check each school’s international admissions page for specifics.

Should I choose a STEM boarding school over a specialized STEM day school?

It depends on priorities. Specialized STEM-only schools may offer deeper subject specialization, while these boarding schools combine strong STEM programs with broad liberal arts, athletics, and residential life — a better fit for students who want STEM depth without narrowing their whole high school experience to one subject area.

Do students at these schools go on to competitive summer research programs?

Many do. Programs like MIT’s Research Science Institute (RSI), MIT PRIMES, and the Summer Science Program are popular next steps for students who have already built a strong research or math foundation at schools like these — though admission to those external programs is separately competitive and not guaranteed by boarding school attendance.

How do I compare STEM facilities between two schools I'm considering?

Request a tour of the science and engineering buildings specifically, ask when they were last renovated, and ask how many independent research projects or theses were completed in the STEM departments in the past year. Facilities age and course catalogs both change over time, so always verify current details rather than relying on older published reviews.

Final overview

The top STEM boarding schools in the USA combine serious science and math infrastructure — research seminars, competition math culture, robotics, and specialized facilities like Choate’s Kohler Environmental Center — with the traditional boarding-school residential experience. Families should verify current course catalogs, research opportunities, and competition results directly with each admissions office, since program strength can shift as faculty and facilities change.
