Top 15 Australian Boarding Schools (2026 Guide)

Top 15 Australian boarding schools for 2026: Geelong Grammar, Melbourne Grammar, King's School, Scotch College, and more, with state curriculum differences and FAQs.

Overview: Like Canada, Australia has no single official ranking of boarding schools. This guide builds a reputation-based top 15 using founding history, boarding capacity, university entrance track record, and international-student support across five states — New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, and South Australia. Because Australia runs state-based senior curricula (HSC, VCE, QCE, WACE, SACE) rather than one national exam, we explain exactly how each state’s system affects university entry before comparing schools.

Families researching Australian boarding schools often assume there is a national exam and a single ranking table, similar to the UK’s A-Levels or the US News rankings. In reality, Australia’s states and territories each run their own senior secondary curriculum and university entrance score (the ATAR is calculated using state-specific results). This guide lists 15 well-known boarding schools across the country’s main boarding states, with the curriculum and location context you need before you compare them.

Why there is no official ranking of Australian boarding schools

Australia’s Department of Education does not publish a national school ranking. Individual states publish HSC/VCE/QCE “top schools” media lists each year based on exam results, but these compare exam performance only — not boarding quality, pastoral care, or international-student support. This guide combines those exam-performance signals with founding history, boarding infrastructure, and reputation to build a broader, more useful comparison.

Victoria’s boarding leaders: Geelong Grammar and Melbourne Grammar

Geelong Grammar School is widely regarded as Australia’s most internationally recognized boarding school, largely due to its Timbertop program — a mandatory year-long wilderness campus for Year 9 students that combines academics with outdoor education. Read our full Geelong Grammar School guide for admissions and fee detail.

Melbourne Grammar School is one of Australia’s oldest boys’ schools, known for a strong rowing tradition and consistently high VCE results feeding into Melbourne’s top universities. See our Melbourne Grammar School guide.

Scotch College (Hawthorn) and Wesley College round out Victoria’s top tier, each offering both day and boarding places with strong co-curricular programs.

New South Wales: King’s, Scots College, Shore, PLC Sydney, Abbotsleigh

The King’s School in North Parramatta, founded in 1831, is Australia’s oldest independent school and one of the largest boarding communities in the country. The Scots College and Shore are Sydney’s other historic boys’ boarding schools, both with strong HSC results and harbourside or North Shore campuses.

On the girls’ side, Abbotsleigh runs a genuine boarding house for regional and international students, while PLC Sydney is a highly regarded day school that supports international families through a homestay program rather than on-campus dormitories.

Queensland, Western Australia, and South Australia

Brisbane Grammar School, Churchie, and The Southport School lead Queensland’s boarding options, with QCE results and strong rugby/rowing programs. Guildford Grammar School is Western Australia’s best-known boarding school, located on a historic riverside campus near Perth. Scotch College Adelaide anchors South Australia’s boarding tier with consistently strong SACE outcomes.

International student support: what to check before applying

  • Student visa (subclass 500): Most international boarding students require an Australian Student visa, and schools typically issue a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) to support the application.
  • Under-18 welfare arrangements: Boarding schools generally act as the designated guardian for boarding students; day schools with homestay programs require an approved homestay welfare arrangement.
  • English language support: Ask whether the school offers an EAL/D (English as an Additional Language) program alongside mainstream classes.
  • University pathway support: Request each school’s recent ATAR distribution and university destination list, especially if you plan to apply outside Australia.

How to choose among the top 15 Australian boarding schools

  1. Match the state curriculum to your university goal — HSC, VCE, QCE, WACE, and SACE all lead to an ATAR but differ in structure and subject scaling.
  2. Confirm boarding type — full residential boarding versus homestay-based day schools.
  3. Check gender policy — several schools on this list (King’s, Scots, Shore, Melbourne Grammar, Scotch College, Brisbane Grammar, Churchie, TSS) remain single-sex boys’ schools; PLC Sydney and Abbotsleigh are girls’ schools.
  4. Ask about outdoor-education requirements — programs like Geelong Grammar’s Timbertop are mandatory and shape the whole school experience.
  5. Compare total annual cost, including flights home during term breaks, before committing.

Frequently asked questions: top Australian boarding schools

Is there an official ranking of Australian boarding schools?

No. Australia has no single national ranking body for K-12 boarding schools. State media publish annual HSC/VCE/QCE exam-result tables, but these measure exam performance only, not boarding quality or pastoral care.

What is the oldest boarding school in Australia?

The King’s School in North Parramatta, NSW, founded in 1831, is Australia’s oldest independent school.

Do all Australian states use the same final exam?

No. New South Wales uses the HSC, Victoria uses the VCE, Queensland uses the QCE, Western Australia uses the WACE, and South Australia uses the SACE. All feed into the national ATAR system for university entry.

What is Geelong Grammar’s Timbertop program?

Timbertop is a mandatory Year 9 wilderness campus program run by Geelong Grammar School, combining outdoor education with academics away from the main Corio campus. It is one of the school’s most distinctive features internationally.

Are PLC Sydney and similar schools actual boarding schools?

PLC Sydney is primarily a day school that supports international students through an approved homestay program rather than on-campus dormitories. Abbotsleigh, by contrast, operates a genuine on-campus boarding house.

How much does an Australian boarding school cost per year?

Full boarding tuition and residence for international students typically runs AUD $70,000–$95,000 per year. Day-plus-homestay arrangements are usually lower, around AUD $35,000–$50,000. Always confirm current fees with each school.

Do international students need a student visa for boarding school in Australia?

Yes, most international boarding students require a Student visa (subclass 500), supported by a Confirmation of Enrolment from the school. Rules can change, so verify current requirements on the official Department of Home Affairs website.

Which of these schools are co-educational?

Geelong Grammar, Wesley College, Haileybury, Scotch College Adelaide, and Guildford Grammar are co-educational. Most others on this list remain single-sex boys’ or girls’ schools.

Final overview

The top 15 Australian boarding schools for 2026 span five states, each with its own senior curriculum and ATAR pathway. Since there is no single official ranking, compare boarding type, curriculum, gender policy, and university placement history directly against your family’s goals before finalizing a shortlist.
