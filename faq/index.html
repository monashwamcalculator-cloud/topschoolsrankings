Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about browsing listings, creating an account, submitting reviews, and managing school profiles.

General Questions

What is Top Schools Rankings?

An independent platform where families browse verified school and university listings, read reviews, and explore admissions guides and rankings worldwide.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Browsing the directory, reading articles, and viewing listing details is completely free.

How do I sign up?

Sign up with your email and password, or use Continue with Google. Phone signup is not required.

Can I review a school?

Any signed-in member can review a listing once. You cannot review your own institution's listing.

For Institutions & Schools

How do I add a new school listing?

Sign in, go to Add Listing, and complete the simple form: institution details, website, contact info, logo, cover image, about text (150-300 words), and courses offered.

What does "Verified" status mean?

It means the listing has all required information complete. Verified status must be refreshed by updating the listing every 60 days.

How do I claim an existing school profile?

Sign in with your account and use Claim this listing on the profile page to manage it from your dashboard.

Can an institution be listed multiple times?

No. Each institution can only be listed once. Duplicates are blocked by matching website URL.

What are the image requirements for school profiles?

Logo and cover image: JPG, PNG, or WEBP. Max 200 KB each.

Editorial & Policies

Can I pay for a better ranking?

No. We provide information only. Rankings and listings are not sold. Contact institutions directly for admissions.

How do I report incorrect information?

Email topschoolsrankings@gmail.com with the page URL, correction, and an official source link. See Contact Us for details.

Can I write for Top Schools Rankings?

Yes - see Write for Us for topics, guidelines, and how to pitch school or university content.

Do you use cookies?

Yes. We use essential cookies for site functionality. Google Analytics (GA4) and Google AdSense load only after you accept cookies via our banner. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How can I manage my cookie preferences?

Use our cookie banner to decline non-essential cookies, or visit Google Ads Settings. You can also clear cookies in your browser at any time.
