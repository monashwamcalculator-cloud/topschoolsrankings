The Appleby College is one of the most recognized independent boarding schools in Canada. Located in Oakville, the school is known for its strong academics, leadership development, global student community, and university preparation programs.

Founded in 1911, Appleby College has built a reputation as a premium private school offering a balanced education experience that combines academics, extracurricular activities, innovation, and personal growth.

Today, students from different parts of the world choose Appleby College because of:

  • Internationally respected academics
  • Boarding opportunities
  • International Baccalaureate (IB) program
  • University preparation support
  • Safe and supportive campus environment

This guide covers everything you need to know about Appleby College for international students.

About Appleby College

The Appleby College is a private co-educational day and boarding school for students in grades 7–12.

Key Highlights

  • Established in 1911
  • Located in Oakville
  • Independent school in Ontario
  • Offers boarding and day school options
  • International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme available
  • Diverse international student community

The school focuses strongly on:
Academic excellence
Leadership development
Global citizenship
Personal growth

Why Appleby College Is Popular Internationally

1. Strong Academic Environment

Appleby College is widely known for maintaining high academic standards.

Students benefit from:

  • Smaller class sizes
  • Personalized learning support
  • Experienced teachers
  • Advanced academic programs

The school encourages students to develop:

  • Critical thinking skills
  • Communication abilities
  • Independent learning habits

2. International Baccalaureate (IB) Program

The Appleby College offers the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IBDP), one of the world’s most respected academic programs.

The IB curriculum helps students prepare for:

  • Canadian universities
  • Universities in the United States
  • International higher education opportunities

Many families choose Appleby specifically because of its university preparatory environment and globally recognized academics.

3. International Student Experience

Appleby College has students from many countries, creating a multicultural learning environment.

International students receive support through:

  • Boarding supervision
  • Academic advising
  • University counseling
  • Student wellness programs

This supportive environment helps students adapt smoothly to life in Canada.

Boarding Life at Appleby College

One of the major highlights of Appleby College is its boarding experience.

Boarding students live on campus in supervised residences that provide:

  • Structured routines
  • Safe accommodation
  • Community engagement
  • Study support

Students also participate in:

  • Evening activities
  • Sports and recreation
  • Leadership programs
  • Cultural events

Boarding life helps students become more independent and responsible.

Appleby College Fees

As one of Canada’s leading independent boarding schools, Appleby College has premium tuition fees.

Estimated Annual Costs:

  • Day school tuition: Higher private-school tuition range
  • Boarding tuition: Generally exceeds CAD $80,000+ annually depending on grade level and services included

Tuition fees may change yearly.

Families should always refer to the official school website for updated fee details.

Appleby College Admission Process

Admission to Appleby College is competitive due to strong demand and limited seats.

Common Admission Requirements:

  • Academic transcripts
  • Student application form
  • Teacher recommendations
  • Interview process
  • English proficiency proof (for some students)

Some applicants may also complete:

  • Entrance assessments
  • Personal essays or statements

Is Appleby College Difficult to Get Into?

Admission is considered selective because the school looks for students who demonstrate:

  • Strong academic ability
  • Leadership potential
  • Positive character
  • Extracurricular involvement

Students with balanced academic and personal profiles often have stronger applications.

Location Advantage: Oakville, Ontario

Oakville is considered one of the most desirable communities in Ontario for families and students.

For international families looking for a prestigious boarding school in Canada, Appleby College is certainly worth exploring.
