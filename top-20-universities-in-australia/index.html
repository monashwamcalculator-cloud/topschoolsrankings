This top 20 universities in Australia guide is built for international students comparing Go8 research brands with technology universities such as UTS and RMIT. For details on how we evaluate institutional metrics, see our Ranking Methodology.

QS 2026 Australia: what changed

Melbourne and UNSW occupy consecutive global ranks (19 and 20). Adelaide University appears as the newly merged institution in QS 2026 reporting. Always verify the latest course CRICOS codes on official sites.

UTS, RMIT and technology-focused campuses

Students targeting design, media, engineering practice, or industry projects often prefer UTS and RMIT even when overall QS trails Melbourne/UNSW.

Group of Eight (Go8) explained for international applicants

The Group of Eight comprises Australia’s leading research universities: Melbourne, UNSW, Sydney, ANU, Monash, Queensland, UWA, and Adelaide University (post-merger). They dominate international search because they appear in QS top 100, offer large course catalogues, and maintain strong employer recognition across Asia-Pacific.

Students targeting computer science should note that Melbourne, Sydney, ANU, Monash, UNSW, UTS, Adelaide, and Queensland have all appeared in global CS subject bands in recent QS subject reporting—another reason to compare programme tables, not only overall university rank.

City costs: Sydney vs Melbourne vs Brisbane vs Perth

  • Sydney: Highest rent; UNSW, Sydney, UTS, Macquarie cluster.
  • Melbourne: Cultural capital; Melbourne, Monash, RMIT, Deakin options.
  • Brisbane: Growing tech hub; Queensland and QUT.
  • Perth: Mining/engineering links; UWA and Curtin.

Scholarship availability varies by faculty—apply early and check faculty-specific international pages.

FAQs: Top universities in Australia

Which Australian university is #1 in QS 2026?

Answer: University of Melbourne at global rank 19.

Is UNSW better than Melbourne?

Answer: Overall QS places Melbourne #19 and UNSW #20—programme fit matters more than one place.

What is Adelaide University in QS 2026?

Answer: QS lists the merged Adelaide University (reported around global #82).

Are all top 20 Go8 members?

Answer: No. Go8 are the research-intensive core; UTS/RMIT/Macquarie etc. expand the practical shortlist.

Best city for international students?

Answer: Sydney and Melbourne dominate options; Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide can offer lower living costs.

Do you cover Australian schools too?

Answer: Yes—see Geelong Grammar and Hills Grammar guides for secondary pathways.

Should I only apply to QS top 100?

Answer: Not necessarily—compare scholarships, major strength, and graduate outcomes.
