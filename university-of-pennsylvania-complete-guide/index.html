History and Legacy

The Benjamin Franklin Foundation

The University of Pennsylvania holds a unique place in American educational history. Founded in 1740 by Benjamin Franklin, it's one of the oldest institutions of higher education in the United States. Franklin's vision was revolutionary – he believed students needed practical education for business, public service, and trade, not just classical languages and theology.

This forward-thinking approach made Penn the first fully fledged multi-faculty university in the USA. While other U.S. colleges focused on traditional liberal arts, Penn pioneered applied learning that prepared students for real-world challenges.

Colonial College Heritage

As one of the nine original Colonial Colleges, Penn was established before the US became a sovereign nation after the American Revolution. This historic status is significant:

  • Founded: 1740 (university status granted 30 years later in 1770)

  • Location: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (urban West Philadelphia)

  • Campus Size: 260-302 acres with 200 buildings

  • Ivy League Member: One of eight prestigious Ivy League schools

  • Association: Founding member of the Association of American Universities

Penn's origins date back to when Benjamin Franklin published his famous essay, "Proposals Relating to the Education of Youth", in 1749. He circulated it among Philadelphia's prominent citizens and organized 24 trustees to form the institution.

Academic Programs and Schools

Four Undergraduate Schools

When applying to the University of Pennsylvania, undergraduates choose one of four undergraduate schools that serve as their "home base":

  1. The College of Arts and Sciences (liberal arts)

  2. The Wharton School (business and economics)

  3. Penn Engineering (School of Engineering and Applied Science)

  4. Penn Nursing (School of Nursing)

Each school offers highly regarded undergraduate programmes with distinct curricula and specialisations.

Graduate and Professional Programs

Penn's graduate and professional programs are housed in 12 schools on one campus, offering:

  • 16,000+ graduate students

  • 300+ degree and certificate programmes

  • Fields ranging from Accounting to Women's Health

Featured Dual-Degree Programs

Penn offers unique coordinated dual-degree programmes:

  • Huntsman Program: International Studies and Business (College + Wharton)

  • Jerome Fisher Program (M&T): Management & Technology (Wharton + Engineering)

  • Nursing and Healthcare Management: (Nursing + Wharton) – BS in Nursing + BS in Economics

These programmes address dynamic changes in healthcare delivery, financing, and management while earning degrees from two prestigious schools.

Why Wharton Stands Out

The Wharton School is the world's first collegiate business school – a notable "first" landmark on Penn's campus. Wharton offers:

  • Global Youth Program

  • Undergraduate business programmes

  • MBA and executive education

  • Broad slate of academic programmes for every career stage

Admissions Requirements

Selectivity and Acceptance Rate

The University of Pennsylvania is one of the most selective universities in the country:

  • Acceptance Rate: ~18%

  • Annual Applications: ~23,000 undergraduate applications

  • Admitted Students: ~4,000 per year

This makes Penn highly competitive, requiring exceptional academic preparation and strong application materials.

Academic Requirements

To have the best shot of getting in, aim for the 75th percentile:

Requirement Target Score
SAT Score 1570 (75th percentile)
ACT Score 36 (75th percentile)
GPA 3.91 or higher
High School Rank Important factor

If your GPA is lower than 3.91, you need to compensate with higher SAT/ACT scores.

Application Requirements

Essential documents include:

  • Common Application (school-specific supplement required)

  • Personal Statement/Essay (required for all freshmen)

  • 2 Letters of Recommendation (Academic Teacher Evaluations)

  • School Report with guidance counselor's recommendation

  • Mid-Year Report and Final Report

  • Home-school Supplement (if applicable)

Not required:

  • Interview

  • Electronic Application (only commonapp)

Application Deadlines

Type Deadline Notification
Regular Admission January 5 March 31

Application Fee $75 (waiver available) -

The Early Decision option is available for students who make UPenn their first choice and agree to matriculate if accepted.

Important Academic Factors

Admissions officials consider these factors very important:

  1. GPA (most important academic factor)

  2. High school class rank (when available)

  3. Letters of recommendation

  4. Personal statement/essay quality

Student Life and Campus

Urban Campus Experience

The University of Pennsylvania is located in downtown West Philadelphia, making it a true urban campus. Key features:

  • 260-acre historic campus in urban Philadelphia

  • College Green: Central hub for student gatherings

  • Students study and socialize on the Green in hammocks during fall and spring

  • 200 buildings across the sprawling campus

Residential Life

First-year requirements:

  • Freshmen required to live on campus

  • Housed in beautiful quad buildings

  • Standard residence hall living creates strong community

After first year:

  • 55% of upperclassmen stay on campus

  • Others go off-campus but usually stay within a block or two

Social Scene and Activities

Penn has a fairly social campus with vibrant activities:

Greek Life:

  • 30-35% of students participate in fraternities/sororities

  • Significant role in student social culture

Extracurricular Opportunities:

  • Cultural, academic, sports, and social clubs

  • Community service organizations

  • Political organizations

  • Extracurrics related to academic interests or hobbies

Events and Entertainment

Never a dull moment at Penn:

  • Penn Quakers games (athletics)

  • Outdoor movie nights

  • Food festivals

  • Concerts

  • Events by Social Planning and Events Committee (SPEC)

Students have myriad options for entertainment through SPEC-organized activities.

Campus Diversity

The student community at UPenn is diverse, with:

  • International students from UK, Australia, Canada, and worldwide

  • Variety of cultural organisations

  • Active wellness commitment

  • intellectually stimulating and socially rewarding environment

FAQs

What is the University of Pennsylvania's acceptance rate?

The University of Pennsylvania has an approximately 18% acceptance rate, making it one of the most selective universities in the country. Each year, Penn receives about 23,000 undergraduate applications and admits roughly 4,000 students. This high selectivity means you need exceptional academic credentials – aim for 1570 SAT, 36 ACT, and 3.91+ GPA – to have a strong chance of admission.

When is the application deadline for University of Pennsylvania?

The regular admission deadline at the University of Pennsylvania is January 5, with notifications by March 31. The Early Decision deadline is November 1, with decisions released December 15. Early Decision is binding – you must matriculate if accepted. The application fee is $75, but fee waivers are available for eligible students.

How much does University of Pennsylvania cost in 2025-2026?

For 2025-2026, University of Pennsylvania undergraduate tuition is $63,204, plus $8,032 in fees (general, technology, clinical), totaling $71,236. With room and board ($19,876), the total cost of attendance is approximately $91,112 per year. This represents a 3.7% tuition increase from the previous year's $60,920.

What programmes is University of Pennsylvania best known for?

The University of Pennsylvania is globally renowned for its Wharton School of Business (world's first collegiate business school, ranked #1 in Business), engineering programmes (Penn Engineering), liberal arts (College of Arts and Sciences), and nursing (ranked #1 globally). Penn also offers unique dual-degree programmes like Management & Technology (Wharton + Engineering) and Nursing & Healthcare Management.

Is University of Pennsylvania good for international students?

Yes, the University of Pennsylvania is excellent for international students from the UK, Australia, Canada, and worldwide. Penn has 16,000+ graduate students and welcomes international undergraduates. The university offers $328 million in financial aid, accepts international applications through Common App, and provides support for diverse cultural backgrounds. The urban Philadelphia campus features a diverse, globally-inclusive student community.

What SAT score do I need for University of Pennsylvania?

To have the best shot of getting into University of Pennsylvania, you should aim for the 75th percentile SAT score of 1570. The average admitted student typically scores between 1510-1570. If your GPA is below 3.91, you'll need to compensate with higher SAT/ACT scores (36 ACT is the 75th percentile equivalent). Penn considers SAT scores very important in admissions decisions.

What is student life like at University of Pennsylvania?

Student life at UPenn is vibrant and engaging, with an active social scene and commitment to wellness. The campus is fairly social, with 30-35% participating in Greek life. Students enjoy College Green (central hub with hammocks), Penn Quakers games, SPEC-organized events (movie nights, food festivals, concerts), and hundreds of cultural, academic, and sports clubs. First years live on campus; 55% of upperclassmen stay. It's intellectually stimulating and socially rewarding.
