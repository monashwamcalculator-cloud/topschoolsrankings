About University of New South Wales

History and Vision

The University of New South Wales was founded in 1949 and has since grown into one of Australia's leading research-intensive universities. Located primarily in Kensington, Sydney, UNSW has a rich history of advancing knowledge and addressing global challenges.

Today, UNSW Sydney boasts:

  • 63,232+ students from 132 countries

  • 7,230 staff in a vibrant, diverse community

  • 334,000+ alumni worldwide

  • 7 faculties covering all major disciplines

  • 47 schools providing world-class education

  • 4 main campuses across Sydney and Canberra

Admission Requirements for International Students

Who Is an International Student?

You're considered an international student if you are NOT:

  • An Australian or New Zealand citizen (including dual citizens)

  • A permanent resident of Australia

  • A holder of a permanent Australian humanitarian visa

Academic Requirements

For Undergraduate Study:

  • You must meet academic entry scores for your chosen degree

  • International direct entry scores are listed on each degree page

  • Admission is based on academic merit

  • Your secondary education qualification must be recognised by UNSW

Entry Options:

  1. Portfolio Entry Early Conditional Offer Scheme – Show talent alongside ATAR

  2. Gateway Admission Pathway – For students from low-socioeconomic areas

  3. UNSW College Diploma – Complete first year in 12 months with lower ATAR

For Postgraduate Study:

  • Generally need a completed undergraduate degree at university level

  • Check specific entry requirements on your chosen degree page

Scholarships & Financial Support

UNSW Offers Over $83 Million in Scholarships Annually

Key Scholarships for International Students

1. International Student Award

  • Value: 20% towards tuition fees for every year of your program

  • Available for: Undergraduate, postgraduate, and UNSW College diploma programs

  • Eligibility: International students from eligible countries commencing in 2026-2027

  • Can be combined with other scholarships

2. Scientia Coursework Scholarship

  • Option 1: Full tuition fee scholarship for minimum program duration

  • Option 2: $20,000 AUD per annum towards tuition fees

  • Based on: Academic merit, leadership, extracurricular activities

3. Lily and Dr Ben KC Chng Scholarship

  • Value: $5,000 per annum for minimum program duration

4. UNSW Law & Justice International Award

  • Value: $10,000 towards tuition fees for 1 year

5. Australia's Global Award

  • Value: $10,000 for one year

  • Payment: Second term of first year

FAQs

1. What is the University of New South Wales best known for?

The University of New South Wales is best known for its engineering programs, business school (AGSM), law faculty, and strong research output. It ranks #20 globally in QS World University Rankings 2026 and is a founding member of Australia's Group of Eight.

2. How much does it cost to study at UNSW for international students?

International students can expect to pay AUD 40,000–55,000 annually for tuition plus AUD 22,000–25,000 for living expenses and AUD 12,000–18,000 for accommodation. Total annual cost ranges from AUD 74,000–98,000.

3. What are the English language requirements for UNSW?

Most programs require IELTS 6.5 overall with no band less than 6.0, though some programs require IELTS 7.0. TOEFL iBT 90 and PTE 64 are also accepted. Test results must be no more than two years old.

4. Does UNSW offer scholarships for international students?

Yes, UNSW offers over $83 million in scholarships annually, including the International Student Award (20% tuition discount), Scientia Scholarship (full tuition or $20,000/year), and various faculty-specific awards.

5. When can I apply to the University of New South Wales?

Applications for 2027 intakes are now open. Some 2026 programs are at capacity and have closed applications. Apply early as demand is high.

6. What accommodation options are available at UNSW?

UNSW offers on-campus accommodation including UNSW Village with fully furnished apartments, colleges with meal plans, and self-catered options. All include 24/7 support and campus facilities access.

7. Is UNSW good for PhD research?

Yes, UNSW offers PhD programs in all faculties with minimum 3-4 year duration. Admission requires a first or upper second class Honours degree. Research focuses on global challenges like climate change, medical treatments, and technology.
