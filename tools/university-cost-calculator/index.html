Enter your figures or complete the checklist. Results are planning estimates, not official decisions.

University is a major investment — annual costs can range from $10,000 at an in-state US public university to over $80,000 at a private elite institution when you include tuition, accommodation, food, transport, and personal expenses. International students often pay 2–3 times more than domestic students.

This University Cost Calculator estimates your total annual expense based on study destination, fee status, institution type, city cost level, and accommodation choice. It's designed for students comparing universities across the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia.

How to use this calculator

  • Select your study destination — tuition structures differ dramatically between countries.
  • Choose your fee status — international students typically pay higher fees than domestic students.
  • Pick the institution type — top-ranked private universities cost more than public or mid-tier schools.
  • Set the city cost level — living in London, New York, or Sydney costs far more than smaller cities.
  • Choose your accommodation — on-campus halls, shared off-campus housing, and solo apartments have very different price points.

Estimate total cost of one academic year at university — tuition plus typical living expenses. Useful for international students comparing study destinations.

Understanding your results

Your estimate includes tuition, accommodation, food, transport, and personal costs. Remember that this is an illustrative figure — actual costs depend on your specific university, city, and lifestyle. Always check the university's official fee page and use their net price calculator if available.

Consider additional costs like textbooks ($500–$1,500/year), health insurance (required for international students in most countries), and travel home during breaks.

Frequently asked questions

International students in the USA typically pay $25,000–$55,000 per year for tuition alone at public universities, and $50,000–$65,000+ at private universities. Total cost of attendance including living expenses ranges from $40,000 to $85,000 per year.

For international students, UK tuition ranges from £15,000 to £45,000 per year, and degrees are typically 3 years (vs 4 in the USA). This can make the total degree cost 20–40% lower in the UK, though living costs in London are comparable to major US cities.

Beyond tuition and rent, budget for textbooks and supplies ($500–$1,500), health insurance ($1,000–$3,000 for international students), technology (laptop, software), social activities, and travel home during breaks. These can add $3,000–$8,000 per year.

How to interpret the result

Use the result to compare scenarios and identify questions. Schools, universities, testing bodies and government agencies may use different scales, rounding rules and eligibility criteria. An estimate on this page cannot override an official calculation.

Before you decide

  • Read the assumptions shown beside the tool.
  • Repeat the calculation with a lower and higher scenario.
  • Save the official requirement that applies to your case.
  • Ask the responsible institution to confirm any conversion in writing.
