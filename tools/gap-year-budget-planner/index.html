Gap Year Budget Planner

Enter your figures or complete the checklist. Results are planning estimates, not official decisions.

A gap year can be transformative — but it needs careful financial planning. Whether you're volunteering abroad, traveling, working, or joining a structured program, costs can range from $5,000 for a low-budget backpacking trip to $30,000+ for premium gap year programs.

This Gap Year Budget Planner helps you estimate total costs including program fees, travel, living expenses, and insurance for your planned time away.

How to use this planner

  • Enter program fees — structured programs (e.g., volunteering placements, language courses) may charge $2,000–$15,000.
  • Add travel costs — flights, trains, and local transport between destinations.
  • Set monthly living costs — accommodation, food, and personal spending in your destination(s).
  • Enter duration — most gap years are 6–12 months, but adjust as needed.

Estimate a 12-month gap year budget .

Understanding your results

Your total includes all entered costs multiplied by duration. Budget an additional 10–15% contingency for unexpected expenses, medical emergencies, or currency fluctuations. Travel insurance (typically $500–$1,500 for a year) is essential and often required by structured programs.

Frequently asked questions

A gap year typically costs $10,000–$25,000 depending on destinations and lifestyle. Budget travelers in Southeast Asia or South America can manage on $8,000–$12,000, while structured programs in Europe or Australia may cost $20,000–$30,000.

Common funding sources include savings from part-time work, family contributions, gap year scholarships (offered by organizations like the Gap Year Association), working holiday visas (available for Australia, New Zealand, UK), and teaching English abroad (which pays $1,000–$2,500/month in many countries).

How to interpret the result

Use the result to compare scenarios and identify questions. Schools, universities, testing bodies and government agencies may use different scales, rounding rules and eligibility criteria. An estimate on this page cannot override an official calculation.

Before you decide

  • Read the assumptions shown beside the tool.
  • Repeat the calculation with a lower and higher scenario.
  • Save the official requirement that applies to your case.
  • Ask the responsible institution to confirm any conversion in writing.
