Boarding School Cost Calculator (2026)

Enter your figures or complete the checklist. Results are planning estimates, not official decisions.

Choosing a boarding school is one of the biggest financial decisions a family can make. Tuition alone can range from $30,000 to over $70,000 per year in the USA, and £25,000 to £50,000+ in the UK. When you add boarding fees, meals, uniforms, trips, and extracurricular activities, the total annual cost often surprises parents.

This Boarding School Cost Calculator gives you a realistic estimate based on your chosen country, school tier, boarding type, and expected extras. Whether you're comparing options in the USA, UK, Canada, or Australia, this tool helps you plan your budget before contacting schools directly.

How to use this calculator

  • Select your country — fee structures vary significantly between US, UK, Canadian, and Australian boarding schools.
  • Choose the school tier — mid-tier regional schools cost less than premium or elite institutions.
  • Pick your boarding type — full boarding is the most expensive; weekly or flexi boarding reduces costs, and day-student fees exclude boarding entirely.
  • Set your extras level — this covers uniforms, sports equipment, school trips, music lessons, and similar add-ons.
  • Click "Calculate estimate" to see a detailed cost breakdown.

Use this calculator to estimate total annual boarding school costs . Figures are illustrative — always confirm current fees on each school's official website.

Understanding your results

The estimate shows a breakdown of tuition, boarding fees, meals, and extras. Keep in mind that actual fees vary between schools — elite institutions like Eton, Phillips Exeter, or The King's School charge significantly more than regional alternatives. Always confirm current fees on each school's official website before making decisions.

Many boarding schools offer financial aid, bursaries, or merit scholarships that can reduce the effective cost by 20–50%. Ask each school's admissions office about available support.

Frequently asked questions

How to interpret the result

Use the result to compare scenarios and identify questions. Schools, universities, testing bodies and government agencies may use different scales, rounding rules and eligibility criteria. An estimate on this page cannot override an official calculation.

Before you decide

  • Read the assumptions shown beside the tool.
  • Repeat the calculation with a lower and higher scenario.
  • Save the official requirement that applies to your case.
  • Ask the responsible institution to confirm any conversion in writing.
