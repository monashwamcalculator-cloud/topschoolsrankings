Study Abroad Budget Planner: 4-Year Degree Cost (2026)

Enter your figures or complete the checklist. Results are planning estimates, not official decisions.

Studying abroad is exciting, but the total cost of a full degree — not just one year — can be staggering. A 4-year undergraduate program in the USA can cost $160,000–$300,000+ all-in, while a 3-year UK degree might run £60,000–£150,000 including living expenses.

This Study Abroad Budget Planner helps you estimate the complete cost of your entire program, including tuition, living expenses, travel, and insurance over multiple years. It gives you the big-picture number that many students overlook when planning.

How to use this planner

  • Select your destination — different countries have different fee structures and living costs.
  • Enter program length — undergraduate is typically 3–4 years, master's is 1–2 years.
  • Enter annual cost — combine tuition and living expenses (use our University Cost Calculator first if unsure).
  • Add travel and insurance — flights home and health insurance add up over multiple years.

Plan a four-year undergraduate budget for studying abroad. Adjust years for master’s programs.

Understanding your results

The total shown is your estimated all-in cost for the entire program. This helps with financial planning, loan applications, and scholarship searches. Remember that costs typically increase 3–5% annually due to inflation, so your final year may cost more than your first.

Many families spread costs using education loans, installment payment plans, or by earning income through part-time work (where visa rules allow). Factor in currency exchange rates if you're paying in a different currency.

How to interpret the result

Use the result to compare scenarios and identify questions. Schools, universities, testing bodies and government agencies may use different scales, rounding rules and eligibility criteria. An estimate on this page cannot override an official calculation.

Before you decide

  • Read the assumptions shown beside the tool.
  • Repeat the calculation with a lower and higher scenario.
  • Save the official requirement that applies to your case.
  • Ask the responsible institution to confirm any conversion in writing.
