How a Backlog Affects Your CGPA

A "backlog" or failed subject usually means you received 0 grade points for those subject credits. However, the credits are still counted in your denominator. This pulls your CGPA down massively.

The silver lining? When you finally clear the backlog, your university generally replaces that 0 with your new passing grade. This means clearing a single high-credit backlog can skyrocket your CGPA overnight.

How to Use This Calculator

  • Current CGPA: The CGPA on your latest transcript which includes the F/backlog grade.
  • Total Credits Completed: The total number of credits your university has graded you on so far (including the failed subject).
  • Credits of the Backlog Subject: Is it a minor 2-credit lab or a massive 4-credit core subject?
  • Expected Grade Point: What grade do you realistically think you can score when you retake the exam? (e.g., 6, 7, 8, 9)

Grade Replacement Policy

This calculator assumes your university uses a standard Grade Replacement Policy. This means the old "Fail" grade is entirely wiped from your CGPA calculation and replaced by your new grade. If your university averages the two attempts instead (rare for Indian engineering colleges but common abroad), your CGPA increase will be exactly half of what is shown above.
