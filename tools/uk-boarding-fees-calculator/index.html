UK boarding schools are renowned worldwide for academic excellence, but fees can be substantial. Annual boarding fees at mid-tier schools typically range from £25,000 to £35,000, while elite schools like Eton College, Harrow, or Winchester can charge £45,000–£50,000+ per year.

This UK Boarding Fees Calculator estimates termly and annual costs based on school tier and entry point, giving parents a clear picture of the financial commitment involved.

How to use this calculator

  • Select school tier — mid-tier boarding schools cost significantly less than elite institutions.
  • Choose entry point — 13+ entry (Year 9) is the most common; sixth form (16+) entry may have different fee structures.

Understanding your results

UK boarding school fees are usually quoted per term (three terms per year). The annual total includes tuition, boarding, and meals. Additional costs for extras like music lessons, sports equipment, school trips, and examination fees can add £2,000–£6,000 per year.

Many UK schools offer means-tested bursaries covering 10–100% of fees. Scholarships for academic excellence, music, art, or sport are also common and can reduce fees by 10–50%.

Frequently asked questions

Eton College's boarding fees are approximately £48,000–£50,000 per year (2025–2026), making it one of the most expensive boarding schools in the UK. However, Eton offers means-tested bursaries that can cover up to 100% of fees.

Standard fees typically include tuition, full boarding (accommodation), meals, use of school facilities, and pastoral care. Extras like music tuition, sports coaching, examination fees, school trips, and laundry are usually charged separately.

Fees are usually paid termly (three times per year) in advance. Most schools offer monthly payment plans through third-party providers. Some schools also offer sibling discounts or early payment discounts.
