The Brearley School stands as one of Manhattan's most prestigious independent all-girls day schools, delivering a rigorous K–12 college-preparatory education on the Upper East Side. Founded in 1884, Brearley cultivates intellectual curiosity, ethical leadership, and resilience in approximately 772–786 students from diverse backgrounds.

Essential School Profile

Brearley maintains two academic buildings on East 83rd Street plus a nearby field house, forming a cohesive urban campus in Yorkville. Small classes averaging 15 students enable personalized instruction from a faculty where 85% hold advanced degrees.

Educational Philosophy and Divisions

Brearley's mission—"to inspire girls of keen intellect and diverse backgrounds to think critically, creatively, and ethically"—guides a sequenced liberal arts curriculum emphasizing depth over breadth. The school rejects rote learning in favor of Socratic seminars, research projects, and interdisciplinary inquiry across three divisions.

Lower School (K–Grade IV)

Playful yet structured foundation in literacy (phonics/systematic reading), mathematics, science inquiry, social studies, Spanish/French, art, music, and PE. Daily writing builds expressive skills early.

Middle School (Grades V–VIII)

Expanded core academics include lab sciences, ancient languages (Latin/Greek options), advanced history, and electives. Advisory programs address adolescent development alongside rising academic demands.

Upper School (Grades IX–XII)

College-level rigor rivals elite public magnets: triple science sequence, mathematics through AP Calculus/Statistics, 20+ AP courses, seminar-style humanities, and capstone independent studies. Heavy emphasis on analytical essays, debates, and ethical case studies.

Brearley integrates ethics discussions, arts, and service-learning throughout, producing graduates with exceptional critical thinking and communication skills.

Campus and Facilities

The 610 E 83rd Street flagship hosts all divisions with state-of-the-art science laboratories, art studios, a comprehensive library, dining hall, and performance spaces. Adjacent at 590 E 83rd Street, recent expansions added flexible classrooms and athletic venues.

A dedicated field house on E 87th Street features regulation gymnasium, fitness center, and team practice areas supporting 14 varsity sports. Yorkville's parks and proximity to museums enable seamless experiential learning excursions.

Tuition, Aid, and Accessibility

2025–26 Tuition: $66,800 (all-inclusive: books, lunch, trips, clubs, sports, arts, technology).

Brearley's groundbreaking affordability program eliminates tuition for families earning ≤$100,000 (no significant assets), covering ~5% of students. Need-based aid meets 100% of demonstrated need for ~20% of families (~$9.4M awarded annually).

Admissions remain need-blind; acceptance rate ~20% via rolling applications, interviews, and assessments.

Student Demographics and Selectivity

Classes draw from 120+ ZIP codes with 56% students of color, reflecting deliberate diversity initiatives. Admission prioritizes academic promise, curiosity, and character over test scores alone.

Co-Curricular Excellence

Athletics field varsity/interscholastic teams in soccer, basketball, volleyball, cross-country, track, tennis, swimming, and more. Arts programs include three orchestras, 20+ annual productions, visual arts exhibitions, and dance ensembles.

Extracurriculars encompass student government, debate, robotics, Model UN, publications (yearbook/newspaper), affinity groups, and 50+ clubs emphasizing leadership and service.

College Outcomes and Alumnae Impact

Brearley boasts near-100% college matriculation, with average SAT 1480/ACT 34 sending graduates to Ivies (Harvard, Yale, Princeton), top liberals arts colleges (Williams, Amherst), and elite universities worldwide.

Distinguished alumnae lead in law (Ruth Bader Ginsburg), journalism (Jill Abramson), arts (Caroline Kennedy), academia, medicine, and public policy—embodying Brearley's vision of "active, responsible citizenship."

Community and Culture

Reviews praise Brearley's nurturing yet demanding environment where teachers serve as mentors. The beaver mascot symbolizes industriousness; traditions like assemblies and inter-grade events foster enduring bonds.
