Winchester College UK: 2026 Admissions, Fees & Boarding Guide

Quick summary: Winchester College is one of England's oldest and most prestigious independent boarding schools, founded in 1382 in Winchester, Hampshire. For 2026–27, boarding fees are £20,700 per term (£62,100 per year). The school uses its own Winchester Entrance exam (not Common Entrance), admits boys at 13+ boarding, and offers co-educational Sixth Form (16+) entry with girls' boarding expanding from 2026.

Winchester College admissions attract international families seeking a top UK boarding education outside the overcrowded "Eton vs Harrow" content space. Founded nearly 650 years ago, Winchester is known for academic rigour, its unique Election scholarship exam, and strong university outcomes — including 39 Oxbridge places and 50 U.S. university offers in a recent cohort, alongside pathways relevant to families also researching UK university admissions and U.S. Ivy League applications.

This guide explains Winchester College fees for 2026–27, 13+ and 16+ entry routes, international visa rules, bursaries, and verified exam results. Confirm all figures on the official Winchester College fees page before applying.

Winchester College Overview: Winchester, Hampshire

Winchester College (informally Winchester or Wincoll) is an independent boarding and day school in Winchester, Hampshire, England. It is one of the oldest continuously operating schools in the world.

  • Founded: 1382 by William of Wykeham, Bishop of Winchester
  • Motto: "Manners Makyth Man"
  • Location: Winchester, Hampshire (historic cathedral city)
  • Ages: Primarily 13–18 (13+ boys boarding; 16+ co-ed Sixth Form)
  • Type: Independent boarding school (HMC member)
  • Headmaster: Dr Ralph Townsend
  • International pupils: Approximately 20% from overseas (industry data)
  • Admissions email: admissions@wincoll.ac.uk | Tel: +44 (0)1962 621 100

Winchester is distinctive for not using the standard Common Entrance exam used by many major UK public schools. Instead, it runs the Winchester Entrance and the elite Election scholarship examination.

Winchester College Admissions Routes (2026 Entry)

13+ entry (Year 9) — boys, full boarding

The main Winchester College admissions route is 13+ entry into Year 9. This pathway is for boys and is predominantly full boarding.

  1. Registration: Complete registration form + non-refundable £480 fee (waived for free-school-meals qualifiers with evidence)
  2. House selection: Families typically engage with housemasters ~2 years before the exam
  3. Pre-assessment: Short test and interview may be required for an offer
  4. Acceptance fee: £2,000 when accepting a Year 9 place by 1 June of Year 6 (credited to Spring Term bill in first year)
  5. Winchester Entrance or Election: Taken in April/May of Year 8 (2026 dates: 27–29 April)
  6. Deposit & contract: Paid in Year 8 on signing the parent contract

11+ via The Pilgrims' School

Boys finishing Year 6 may enter The Pilgrims' School at 11+, with progression to Winchester College at 13+. This is a dedicated feeder pathway for families planning early.

16+ entry (Sixth Form) — boys and girls, day and boarding

Winchester College admissions at 16+ are co-educational, accepting both boys and girls for Sixth Form as day or boarding pupils. Girls' boarding capacity has expanded, with dedicated boarding houses developing from 2026 onward.

Sixth Form applicants follow the separate 16+ registration process published on the school's admissions pages.

Winchester Entrance Exam vs Election: What Makes Winchester Different

Unlike Eton, Harrow, and many HMC schools that rely on Common Entrance (CE), Winchester uses its own assessments.

Exam Who takes it Purpose
Winchester Entrance Most 13+ candidates Standard academic admissions test in Year 8
Election Invited high-achievers Prestigious academic scholarship exam (replaces Entrance)

Winchester Entrance (standard 13+ exam)

All candidates must take papers in:

  • Mathematics
  • English
  • Science
  • History
  • Geography

Maths and English papers are shared with Election. The school states that for state-sector and overseas candidates, Maths and English are the prime requirements, though the full paper set is expected.

Exams are typically held over three days in late April of Year 8 at the candidate's school or an approved centre.

Election (academic scholarship)

Election is Winchester's historic scholarship examination — taken instead of Winchester Entrance by invited candidates. It includes:

  • Written papers over three days (April 2026: 27–29 April)
  • Mandatory English, Maths 1, Science, General Paper 1
  • Optional papers: Latin, French, Greek, History, Geography, Maths 2, General Paper 2
  • Two interviews assessing scholarly qualities

Successful candidates become Scholars or Exhibitioners. Scholarships provide free individual tuition for sport/music awards but do not automatically waive school fees — means-tested bursaries are separate.

Financial Support, Bursaries and Scholarships

Winchester College operates one of the UK's more generous means-tested bursary programmes:

  • 140+ pupils receive means-tested financial support annually
  • Bursaries range from 5% to 100% of fees based on family need
  • Financial support must be requested at registration
  • Additional help available for school trips and co-curricular activities
  • Academic, Music, and Sport Awards recognise exceptional talent (awards do not automatically reduce tuition)

Historically, Winchester linked scholarships to fee remission; this has changed — today, bursaries are means-tested and scholarships are primarily honorary with subject-specific benefits.

What Is "Div"? Winchester's Unique Academic Culture

Winchester College is famous for Div — a distinctive non-examined liberal arts programme encouraging original thinking, debate, and intellectual curiosity beyond standard GCSE/A-Level syllabuses. This culture aligns with the school's branding: "Original Thinking Since 1382."

Div reflects Winchester's philosophy that education is broader than examination grades — though the school's exam outcomes remain among the strongest nationally.

International Students: Visas, English, and Support

Winchester College welcomes international pupils (~20% of the student body). Key requirements:

  • Child Student Visa or Student Visa where applicable — school charges £1,440/year for visa sponsorship support
  • Non-UK deposit: £20,700 (vs £4,000 for UK residents)
  • English: ~9% of pupils have English as an additional language; EAL support provided individually
  • Entrance exam: Overseas candidates take full Winchester Entrance papers; Maths and English weighted most heavily
  • Duolingo/IELTS/TOEFL: May be considered for Sixth Form international applicants (check current 16+ guidance)

International families should begin applications early — house places and visa processing add lead time beyond UK domestic applicants.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How much does Winchester College cost in 2026–27?
Boarding is £20,700 per term (£62,100 per year). Day fees are £15,318 per term (£45,954 per year), plus registration, deposit, laptop, exams, and optional extras.

Does Winchester College use Common Entrance?
No. Winchester uses its own Winchester Entrance examination. High-achieving candidates may be invited to Election instead — the school's scholarship exam.

Is Winchester College only for boys?
At 13+, boarding is for boys. At 16+ Sixth Form, Winchester admits both boys and girls as day and boarding pupils. Girls' boarding has expanded from 2026.

What grades do you need for Winchester College?
There is no fixed public grade threshold — admission depends on Winchester Entrance or Election performance, interview, and house placement. The school assesses academic potential, not just prior school labels.

Does Winchester offer financial aid?
Yes. Means-tested bursaries support 140+ pupils annually, from 5% to 100% fee remission. Request bursary consideration at registration.

What are Winchester College's GCSE and A-Level results?
In 2025: 93% GCSE grades 7–9 and 75% A-Level grades A*–A. The school sent 39 pupils to Oxbridge and 50 to U.S. universities in a recent cohort.

Can international students board at Winchester College?
Yes. Approximately 20% of pupils are international. Non-UK families pay a £20,700 deposit and £1,440/year visa support fee if the school sponsors their Child Student Visa.

What is Election at Winchester College?
Election is Winchester's prestigious 13+ scholarship examination — a three-day written and interview assessment taken instead of Winchester Entrance by invited candidates. Scholars and Exhibitioners gain recognition and certain tuition benefits.

Final Thoughts: Is Winchester College Right for Your Child?

Winchester College admissions suit academically curious boys (and Sixth Form girls) who thrive in a rigorous boarding environment with centuries of tradition. The school's own entrance system, Div culture, and outstanding 2025 results — 93% top GCSE grades and 75% A*–A at A-Level — make it a genuine alternative to Eton and Harrow for families researching elite UK boarding schools.

Start early, visit on an open day, request bursary forms at registration if needed, and prepare thoroughly for Winchester Entrance or Election. The right fit depends on your child's love of learning, boarding readiness, and long-term university ambitions — in the UK, U.S., or beyond.
